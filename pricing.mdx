## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. Every new account starts with a **7-day free trial** with full Pro features. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime from your [account settings](https://chat.lindy.ai). Changes take effect immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I hit my usage limit?">
    Lindy will pause and check in with you before continuing resource-heavy tasks. You stay in control. [Learn more about how usage works](/account-billing/usage).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused resources roll over?">
    No. Your plan resets every billing cycle (monthly from your signup date). Unused resources do not carry over.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's included in Enterprise?">
    Enterprise includes everything in Max plus team settings, SSO/SCIM/audit logs, HIPAA compliance with a signed BAA, dedicated support, and hands-on onboarding. [Contact sales](mailto:sales@lindy.ai) to learn more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    Lindy accepts all major credit and debit cards through Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
