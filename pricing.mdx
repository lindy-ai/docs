## How Credits Work

Credits are shared across the whole workspace. Each user you add contributes their plan's allocation to the team pool, and admins can set a per-user allocation from there.

Admins can top up workspace credits any time, in increments of **$10 per 1,000 credits**, shared across everyone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does billing work?">
    You pay per user, per month. A person becomes a paid seat when they sign up to Lindy, join your workspace by signing up, or @mention Lindy in your team's Slack for the first time. See [Billing](/coming-soon/lindy-teammate-billing) for the full lifecycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. The first time someone new @mentions Lindy in your team's Slack, they get their first week free before the seat converts to paid. An admin can remove the seat any time with one click.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do credits work across a team?">
    Credits are pooled at the workspace level. Each seat adds its plan's allocation to the pool, and admins can cap any one person's draw. Top up shared credits any time at $10 per 1,000.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans?">
    Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time from your [account settings](https://chat.lindy.ai). Changes take effect immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    No. Your plan resets every billing cycle. Unused credits don't carry over.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    Lindy accepts all major credit and debit cards through Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
