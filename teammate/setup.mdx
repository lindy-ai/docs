Connect your accounts and tools so Lindy can work for you. Invite your colleagues to work together, and add Lindy to Slack if that's where your team communicates.

## 1. Connect your accounts

If you're joining an existing team, accept your workspace invitation before signing up.

| Account | How to connect |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Connect your Google or Microsoft email for inbox management and replies. |
| **Calendar** | Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar for scheduling, meeting prep, recording, and follow-ups. |
| **Phone** | Get updates from your executive assistant and hand off work on the go through iMessage, SMS, or voice notes. |
| **Slack** | Install the Slack app from **Integrations** to work with Lindy in DMs and shared channels. |
| **MS Teams** | Coming soon. |

## 2. Invite your team

An owner or admin clicks **Invite colleague**, or opens **Settings → Members and groups → Add member**. Enter your colleagues' email addresses, choose their roles and plans, and click **Send invite**.

Each person accepts their invitation and connects their own accounts. See [Team Setup](/start-here/team-setup) for the full invitation and onboarding steps.

## 3. Connect your tools

Open **Integrations** and connect the CRM, file storage, project tracker, and other apps Lindy needs for your work.

Personal connections are available to you. Owners and admins can share connections with the workspace so the team can use them. A shared connection uses the connected account's permissions. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for connection settings and sharing.

## 4. Give Lindy a task

Ask privately through iMessage, email, or Slack, or @mention Lindy in a shared channel. Start with work you want finished:

- *"Prepare a brief for tomorrow's client meeting using our previous calls and emails."*
- *"Turn this week's sales calls into a report and update the CRM with next steps."*

You don't need a new channel for Lindy. @mention it in any channel your team already uses, or DM it for anything private.

### Find Lindy and DM it

Press **⌘ + K** (**Ctrl + K** on Windows) to open Slack search, type **Lindy**, and select **Lindy · App**. That opens your DM with Lindy. Anything you share there stays between you and Lindy.

### Where Lindy replies

- **DM the bot** to start or continue your own rolling task. Replies come back in the DM.
- **@mention Lindy in a channel or thread** and it replies inside that thread, never as a new top-level message. Invite the bot to the channel first if it isn't there. Once it's replied, a clear follow-up can continue the thread without re-@mentioning.
- **Auto-join:** the bot can join public channels when linked teammates join, and leave when the last one does. This is configurable.

## What Lindy can do

- **In shared channels and threads:** web search, image generation, public Slack search, and Notion (if your team has connected it). Lindy keeps answers public-safe: it summarizes rather than pasting private data into a public channel.
- **In your DMs and your own threads:** the full toolset, including calendar scheduling, email, SMS, reminders, and meeting reads.
- **Extras:** private summaries when you're pulled into a long thread, and follow-up nudges for asks that haven't gotten a reply.

## Guardrails and approvals

Choose which changes Lindy can make automatically and which need your approval in shared Slack threads. Manage these settings in [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
