Lindy Teammate brings Lindy into your team's Slack. Everyone gets their own private Lindy that works in their DMs, and the whole team shares one that works in channels. Same Lindy, two surfaces.

## How to Set It Up

<Note>
  Open Lindy, click your **workspace name** in the top-left, and choose **Settings**. In the left sidebar, click **Lindy Teammate**. Workspace owners and admins set this up; everyone else can see its status and connect their own personal Lindy.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Click **Connect Slack** and approve access. This is how Lindy shows up for your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Lindy Teammate">
    Click **Create Lindy Teammate** to set up your workspace's shared Lindy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write its intro">
    Set the message Lindy posts to introduce itself when it lands in a channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Lindy does for you

Each person gets their own Lindy, working privately in their Slack DMs. It reaches out on its own to keep you ahead of your day:

- **Daily brief**: a morning rundown of your calendar, important email, and what needs attention
- **Meeting prep and recaps**: a briefing before each meeting, notes and action items after
- **Email alerts**: a nudge when something time-sensitive lands in your inbox
- **Open loops**: when someone asks you for something in Slack and it goes unanswered, Lindy nudges you so it doesn't slip
- **Catch-ups**: when you're @mentioned in a busy channel, Lindy DMs you a short summary of what you missed

These are private to you. They draw on your own email, calendar, and meetings, and no one else on the team sees them.

## What Lindy does for your team

The shared Lindy works out in your channels, where the whole team can see it. @mention it in an approved channel and it gets to work:

- **Answers from your team's sources**, with a link to where each answer came from
- **Drafts the work you need**: client updates, summaries, documents, from a channel and a folder you point it at
- **Team routines**: recurring jobs it runs on a schedule for the whole workspace

For example, ask it to draft a client update from a channel and a Drive folder:

<Steps>
  <Step title="You ask in the channel">
    "Lindy, from #client-acme and the Acme Drive folder, draft this week's client update."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy confirms its sources">
    It names what it can use, so you can see where the answer will come from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy drafts and checks in">
    The draft is ready with source links. Before anything goes to the client, Lindy asks you to **Approve send**, **Ask for a revision**, or **Cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy sends and leaves a record">
    Once you approve, it sends and links the record of what went out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nothing in your approved sources backs up an answer, Lindy says it doesn't know instead of guessing. The shared Lindy sticks to team sources: for anything private, like your own email or calendar, ask your personal Lindy in a DM.

## Approvals keep you in control

The shared Lindy prepares work on its own, then waits for a named, authorized person before it does anything with outside impact:

- Sending an email or text
- Posting into another Slack channel
- Sharing or publishing a doc
- Creating or updating a ticket
- Running code that writes or reaches the network

Read-only lookups from sources your team already approved don't need a click.

## Where Lindy can see and act

| Channel type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Public channels** | Lindy can see and act once it's in your workspace |
| **Private channels** | A channel manager must invite the Lindy app before it can see the channel |
| **Archived channels** | Lindy doesn't view these |
| **Slack Connect, shared channels, guests** | Not supported yet |

## Personal Lindy and the team's Lindy

| | Personal Lindy | The team's Lindy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where** | Your private DMs | Shared channels |
| **Who sees it** | Only you | The whole team |
| **What it uses** | Your email, calendar, meetings, and memory | Approved team sources |
| **What it does** | Briefs, meeting prep, alerts, nudges | Answers, drafts, team routines |

Everyone shares the team's Lindy. Your personal Lindy is yours alone, and its private context never leaks into a shared channel.

## Configure Lindy Teammate

Tune how the shared Lindy works from the settings page:

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Team sources** | The Slack channels and Drive folders Lindy can draw on |
| **Guardrails** | Which actions need approval first: sending email, sharing docs, updating tickets |
| **Roles and seats** | Who can approve external actions: an account lead, an admin, or the requester's manager |
| **Memory** | The facts your team wants Lindy to remember, and which source wins when two disagree |
| **Integrations** | Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Gmail, and Calendar for the whole team |

## Billing

Lindy Teammate is billed per person, as **Teammate Seats**. The first time someone new @mentions Lindy in Slack, they get a free week before their seat converts to paid. See [Billing](/teammate/lindy-teammate-billing) for the full picture.
