## Your inbox

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up inbox triage" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/04-inbox.jpg" href="/academy/04-inbox">
    Turn on Email labeling and Email drafting, add a label, and build a weekday inbox routine. 66 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get drafts that sound like you" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/05-voice.jpg" href="/academy/05-voice">
    Set drafting instructions, check learned instructions, and add your signature. 37 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Set up your morning brief" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/06-brief.jpg" href="/academy/06-brief">
    Choose what your daily brief covers, which calendars it reads, where it arrives, and when. 50 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get alerts for urgent emails" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/07-urgent.jpg" href="/academy/07-urgent">
    Describe what counts as urgent, what to ignore, and where alerts reach you. 39 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow up on unanswered emails" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/08-bumps.jpg" href="/academy/08-bumps">
    Turn on Follow-up bumps and choose how long to wait for a reply. 31 s
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Your meetings

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Record and summarize meetings" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/09-notes.jpg" href="/academy/09-notes">
    Turn on Meeting recording, name the notetaker, set what the summary includes, and find the notes. 46 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get prepped before every call" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/10-prep.jpg" href="/academy/10-prep">
    Turn on Meeting prep, choose which meetings, and set when and where prep arrives. 44 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule meetings without the back-and-forth" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/11-scheduling.jpg" href="/academy/11-scheduling">
    Set your meeting hours and defaults, then hand off a booking. 35 s
  </Card>
  <Card title="Turn calls into follow-ups" img="/lindy-brand-assets/academy/12-followups.jpg" href="/academy/12-followups">
    Choose who gets the recap and what Lindy drafts after a recorded call. 41 s
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
