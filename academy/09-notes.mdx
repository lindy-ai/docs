**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. **Open Meeting recording**: Open Routines, then Meeting recording. Make sure your calendar is connected.
2. **Choose which calls to record**: Switch it on, and choose which calls Lindy joins. All of them, internal, or external.
3. **Make the notetaker recognizable**: Give the notetaker a name people recognize. It joins visibly, so let everyone know you're recording.
4. **Ask for useful notes**: Tell it what you want back. Decisions, action items with owners, and deadlines.
5. **Find your recording and notes**: After the call, open Meetings and pick the call.
6. **Read the notes**: Summary, action items, transcript, and recording, all in one place.
