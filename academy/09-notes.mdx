**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meeting recording">
    Open **Routines**, then **Meeting recording**. Make sure your calendar is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which calls to record">
    Switch it on, and under **Join meetings**, choose which calls Lindy joins: all, internal only, or external only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the notetaker recognizable">
    Set a **Notetaker display name** people will recognize. The notetaker joins visibly, so let everyone know you're recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for useful notes">
    In **Instructions**, say what you want back, like *decisions, action items with owners, and deadlines*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your recording and notes">
    After the call, open **Meetings** in the sidebar and pick the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the notes">
    The summary, action items, transcript, and recording are all in one place.
  </Step>
</Steps>
