**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, then Meeting recording. Make sure your calendar is connected.
2. Switch it on, and choose which calls Lindy joins. All of them, internal, or external.
3. Give the notetaker a name people recognize. It joins visibly, so let everyone know you're recording.
4. Tell it what you want back. Decisions, action items with owners, and deadlines.
5. After the call, open Meetings and pick the call.
6. Summary, action items, transcript, and recording, all in one place.
