**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

1. **Open Follow-up bumps**: Open Routines and find Follow-up bumps.
2. **Choose the right inbox**: Open it, pick your inbox, and switch it on.
3. **Set how long to wait**: Choose how long to wait. Three days is a good start.
4. **Review before sending**: When someone goes quiet, a follow-up waits in your drafts. Read it, tweak it, send it.
   Example: `Help me review follow-up drafts for unanswered client emails. Keep the tone friendly and concise.`
