**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Follow-up bumps">
    Open **Routines** and find **Follow-up bumps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right inbox">
    Open it, pick your inbox, and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how long to wait">
    Choose how long to wait for a reply. Three days is a good start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before sending">
    When someone goes quiet, a follow-up waits in your drafts folder. Read it, edit it, and send it.

    For example: *"Help me review follow-up drafts for unanswered client emails. Keep the tone friendly and concise."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
