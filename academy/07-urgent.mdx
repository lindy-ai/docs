**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Time-sensitive email alerts">
    Open **Routines**, then **Time-sensitive email alerts**. Pick the inbox to watch, and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Be specific about urgency">
    In **Instructions**, say what counts as urgent, like *a client who needs an answer today*. Then say what to ignore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an available channel">
    Under **Delivery channel**, choose where alerts arrive. Text needs a verified phone, and Slack DM needs your Slack account linked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the filter">
    Test it: ask Lindy what would have triggered an alert recently, and why.

    For example: *"Test my urgent email alert criteria against recent email. Show what would trigger, what would be skipped, and why. Do not send any alerts for this test."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
