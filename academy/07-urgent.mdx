**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

1. **Open Time-sensitive email alerts**: Open Routines, then Time-sensitive email alerts. Pick the inbox to watch.
2. **Be specific about urgency**: Say what counts as urgent, like a client who needs an answer today. Then say what to ignore.
3. **Choose an available channel**: Choose where alerts land. Text needs a verified phone. Slack needs your account linked.
4. **Check the filter**: Then test it. Ask Lindy what would have pinged you this week, and why.
