**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. **Open Meeting prep**: Open Routines, then Meeting prep, and switch it on for your work calendar.
2. **Choose the meetings that matter**: Choose all meetings, or just the external ones.
3. **Describe your ideal prep**: In Prep instructions, ask for client context, recent conversations, open commitments, and three questions to ask.
4. **Choose when and where it arrives**: Pick how many minutes before the call it arrives, and where: Lindy chat, email, or Slack.
5. **Try an on-demand prep**: Need it right now? Just ask: prep me for my next client call.
