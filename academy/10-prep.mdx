**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, then Meeting prep, and switch it on for your work calendar.
2. Choose all meetings, or just the external ones.
3. In Prep instructions, ask for client context, recent conversations, open commitments, and three questions to ask.
4. Pick how many minutes before the call it arrives, and where. Lindy chat, email, or Slack.
5. Need it right now? Just ask.

   Example: `Prep me for my next client call. Include recent conversations, open commitments, and three questions to ask.`
