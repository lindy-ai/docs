**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meeting prep">
    Open **Routines**, then **Meeting prep**, and switch it on for your work calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the meetings that matter">
    Choose **All meetings** or **External meetings only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your ideal prep">
    In **Prep instructions**, ask for client context, recent conversations, open commitments, and three questions to ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when and where it arrives">
    Pick how many minutes before the call prep arrives, and where: Lindy chat, email, text, or Slack DM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try an on-demand prep">
    Need it right now? Just ask.

    For example: *"Prep me for my next client call. Include recent conversations, open commitments, and three questions to ask."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
