**Personal**: these settings apply to your own recorded calls.

## Steps

1. **Open recording follow-up settings**: Open Routines, then Meeting recording. These settings kick in after each recorded call.
2. **Choose who gets the recap**: Choose who gets the recap: nobody, internal people, or everyone on the call, client included.
3. **Describe the next action**: Then say what happens next. Start simple: draft a follow-up email to the client.
4. **Review notes before a write**: After the call, open the meeting. Check the names, decisions, and dates.
5. **Ask for a focused follow-up**: Or use Chat with this meeting: draft the client follow-up, with owners and dates.
