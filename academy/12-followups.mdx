**Personal**: these settings apply to your own recorded calls.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open recording follow-up settings">
    Open **Routines**, then **Meeting recording**. These settings apply after each recorded call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets the recap">
    Under **Email recap to participants**, choose who else gets the recap: no one, internal people only, or everyone on the call, client included. You always get your own recap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the next action">
    In **Follow up actions**, say what happens next. Start simple: *draft a follow-up email to the client*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the notes first">
    After the call, open the meeting. Check the names, decisions, and dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a focused follow-up">
    Or use **Chat with this meeting** to draft the client follow-up, with owners and dates.

    For example: *"Draft a client follow-up from this meeting. Include decisions, next steps, owners, and dates. Keep it ready for my review; do not send it."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
