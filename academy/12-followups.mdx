**Personal**: these settings apply to your own recorded calls.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, then Meeting recording. These settings kick in after each recorded call.
2. Choose who else gets the recap. no participants, internal people, or everyone on the call, client included. You always get yours.
3. Then say what happens next. Start simple. draft a follow-up email to the client.
4. After the call, open the meeting. Check the names, decisions, and dates.
5. Or use Chat with this meeting.

   Example: `Draft a client follow-up from this meeting. Include decisions, next steps, owners, and dates. Keep it ready for my review; do not send it.`
