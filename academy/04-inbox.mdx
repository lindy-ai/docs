**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Routines">
    Open **Routines** and choose **Personal**. The built-in routines are listed here. Start with **Email labeling**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what stays in your inbox">
    Turn it on and pick your inbox. Labels under **Keep these in my inbox** stay visible. Labels under **Move these out of my inbox** get archived for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teach Lindy a label">
    Add your own label: give it a name, a color, and instructions in plain English, like *emails from customers about orders, quotes, or bookings*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your reply style">
    Turn on **Email drafting**. Describe your tone and add your signature. Drafts land in your drafts folder, and nothing sends without you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your own routine">
    Click **New** and describe the routine you want.

    For example: *"Every weekday at 8 AM, tell me which emails need a reply and which drafts are ready."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="When, what, where">
    Lindy fills in when it runs, what it does, and where the result goes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it with a dry run">
    Before it runs for the first time, ask for a dry run. Lindy shows exactly what it would do, and sends nothing.

    For example: *"Dry-run my weekday inbox routine."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
