**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

1. **Open Routines**: Open Routines and choose Personal. These are built in. Start with Email labeling.
2. **Choose what stays in your inbox**: Turn it on and pick your inbox. Labels under Keep these in my inbox stay visible. Labels under Move these out get archived for you.
3. **Teach Lindy a label**: Add your own label. Give it a name, a color, and instructions in plain English, like emails from customers about orders, quotes or bookings.
4. **Set your reply style**: Next, turn on Email drafting. Describe your tone and add your signature. Drafts land in your drafts folder, and nothing sends without you.
5. **Make your own routine**: Now make one of your own. Click New and describe it: every weekday at eight, tell me which emails need a reply.
6. **When, what, where**: Lindy fills in when it runs, what to do, and where the result goes. That's a routine.
7. **Test it with a dry run**: Before you trust it, ask for a dry run. Lindy shows exactly what it would do, and sends nothing.
