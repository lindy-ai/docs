**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

1. Open Routines and choose Personal. These are built in. Start with Email labeling.
2. Turn it on and pick your inbox. Labels under Keep these in my inbox stay visible. Labels under Move these out get archived for you.
3. Add your own label. Give it a name, a color, and instructions in plain English, like emails from customers about orders, quotes or bookings.
4. Next, turn on Email drafting. Describe your tone and add your signature. Drafts land in your drafts folder, and nothing sends without you.
5. Now make one of your own. Click New and describe it.

   Example: `Every weekday at 8 AM, tell me which emails need a reply and which drafts are ready.`
6. Lindy fills in when it runs, what to do, and where the result goes.
7. Before you trust it, ask for a dry run. Lindy shows exactly what it would do, and sends nothing.

   Example: `Dry-run my weekday inbox routine.`
