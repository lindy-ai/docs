**Personal**: reminders reach you in Lindy chat, by text, or in Slack.

## Steps

1. **Say what and when**
   Example: `Remind me Friday at 9am Pacific to send Acme the revised proposal.`
2. **Use a condition for a follow-up**: Or make it conditional.
   Example: `Remind me Wednesday at 3pm Pacific if Jamie has not replied about the Acme proposal.`
3. **Check the timing and channel**: Ask for your upcoming reminders anytime, with the time and where each one reaches you.
   Example: `List my upcoming reminders with their dates, times, and delivery channels.`
4. **Change or cancel in plain language**: Plans change? Just say so.
   Example: `Move my Acme proposal reminder to Monday at 10am Pacific.`
