**Personal**: reminders reach you in Lindy chat or by text.

## Steps

1. **Say what and when**: Say what and when. Remind me Friday at nine Pacific to send Acme the revised proposal.
2. **Use a condition for a follow-up**: Or make it conditional. Remind me Wednesday at three if Jamie hasn't replied.
3. **Check the timing and channel**: Ask for your upcoming reminders anytime, with the time and where each one reaches you.
4. **Change or cancel in plain language**: Plans change? Just say so. Move my Acme reminder to Monday at ten.
