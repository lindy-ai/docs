**Personal**: reminders reach you in Lindy chat, by text, or in Slack.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what and when">
    Tell Lindy what to remind you about, and when.

    For example: *"Remind me Friday at 9am Pacific to send Acme the revised proposal."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a condition for a follow-up">
    Or make it conditional.

    For example: *"Remind me Wednesday at 3pm Pacific if Jamie has not replied about the Acme proposal."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timing and channel">
    Ask for your upcoming reminders anytime, with the time and where each one reaches you.

    For example: *"List my upcoming reminders with their dates, times, and delivery channels."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or cancel in plain language">
    Plans change? Just say so.

    For example: *"Move my Acme proposal reminder to Monday at 10am Pacific."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
