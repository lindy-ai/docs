**Personal and Team**: your **Personal** skills are yours. **Workspace** skills are shared with the team and managed by admins.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Skills">
    Open **Skills** in the sidebar. **Personal** skills are yours. **Workspace** skills are shared with everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the process once">
    In chat, describe the process once and ask Lindy to save it as a skill.

    For example: *"Save a personal skill for client updates. When I ask for an Acme update, use its recent meeting notes and client brief, lead with decisions and blockers, then list next steps with owners. Keep it under one page."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the instructions and description">
    Lindy saves it as a skill. Check the description: that's how Lindy knows when to use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it and refine it">
    Next time, just ask for the work. Lindy picks the skill on its own.

    For example: *"Prepare a concise Acme client update. Leave it ready for my review."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
