**Personal and Team**: your skills are yours. Team skills are shared with the workspace and managed by admins.

## Steps

1. **Open Skills**: If Skills is in your sidebar, open it. Yours are personal. Team skills are shared with everyone.
2. **Describe the process once**: In chat, ask Lindy to save a skill.
   Example: `Save a personal skill for client updates. When I ask for an Acme update, use its recent meeting notes and client brief, lead with decisions and blockers, then list next steps with owners. Keep it under one page.`
3. **Check the instructions and description**: Lindy saves it as a skill. Check the description. That's how it knows when to use it.
4. **Try it and refine it**: Next time, just ask for the Acme update. Lindy picks the skill on its own.
   Example: `Prepare a concise Acme client update using the client-update process. Leave it ready for my review.`
