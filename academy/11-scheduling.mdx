**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meeting scheduling">
    Open **Routines**, then **Meeting scheduling**. Switch it on, and check which calendar it uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your meeting windows">
    Set the days and hours you take meetings, plus any rules, like *no calls over lunch*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the normal meeting length">
    Pick your usual meeting length, your video link, and a daily limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate a specific meeting">
    Hand off a booking, and check the proposed times before Lindy sends them.

    For example: *"Find time for a 30-minute call with Jamie from Acme next week. Respect my meeting hours and show me the proposed times first."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
