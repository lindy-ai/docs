**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. **Open Meeting scheduling**: Open Routines, then Meeting scheduling. Check which calendar it uses.
2. **Set your meeting windows**: Set the days and hours you take meetings, plus your rules, like no calls over lunch.
3. **Set the normal meeting length**: Pick your usual length, your video link, and a daily limit.
4. **Delegate a specific meeting**: Then hand it off: Check the proposed times, and Lindy does the rest.
   Example: `Find time for a 30-minute call with Jamie from Acme next week. Respect my meeting hours and show me the proposed times first.`
