**Personal**: these settings apply to your own calendar.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, then Meeting scheduling. Check which calendar it uses.
2. Set the days and hours you take meetings, plus your rules, like no calls over lunch.
3. Pick your usual length, your video link, and a daily limit.
4. Then hand it off. Check the proposed times, and Lindy does the rest.

   Example: `Find time for a 30-minute call with Jamie from Acme next week. Respect my meeting hours and show me the proposed times first.`
