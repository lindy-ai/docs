**Personal and Team**: Recent learnings and Personal files are yours. Team files are shared with the workspace, and only owners and admins can edit them.

## Steps

1. **Brief Lindy like a new assistant**: Brief Lindy like a new hire. Your role, your top client, your hours and time zone.
   Example: `I run client delivery at a marketing agency. Acme is my priority this month. I work 9 to 5 Pacific. Keep updates short, with a clear next action.`
2. **Find what Lindy learned**: On Home, Recent learnings shows what it saved about you. Open one to check it.
3. **Add a useful reference file**: For client work, open Memory and drop in a client brief or brand guide.
4. **Open and verify the file**: It's all plain files. Open one, fix anything that's out of date, and save.
5. **Ask a grounded question**: Now ask for real work. Summarize Acme's audience and approved messaging from the brief, then check it against the file.
   Example: `Use the Acme client brief to summarize the target audience, approved messaging, and anything we should avoid. Point me to the supporting sections.`
