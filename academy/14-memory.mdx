**Personal and Team**: Recent learnings and your **Personal** files are yours. **Workspace** files are shared with the team, and only owners and admins can edit them.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Brief Lindy like a new assistant">
    Tell Lindy about your role, your top client, your hours, and your time zone.

    For example: *"I run client delivery at a marketing agency. Acme is my priority this month. I work 9 to 5 Pacific. Keep updates short, with a clear next action."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what Lindy learned">
    On **Home**, **Recent learnings** shows what Lindy saved about you. Open one to check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a useful reference file">
    For client work, open **Memory** and drop in a client brief or brand guide. Owners and admins can add it under **Workspace**, so everyone's Lindy works from the same brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open and verify the file">
    Everything in Memory is a plain file. Open one, fix anything that's out of date, and click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a grounded question">
    Now ask for real work, and check the answer against the file.

    For example: *"Use the Acme client brief to summarize the target audience, approved messaging, and anything we should avoid. Point me to the supporting sections."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
