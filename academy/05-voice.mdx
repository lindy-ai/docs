**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email drafting">
    Open **Routines**, then **Email drafting**. Pick your inbox and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give clear writing instructions">
    In **Drafting instructions**, say how you write, like *short, friendly, plain language, and no drafts for newsletters*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine what Lindy has learned">
    Lindy also learns from how you write. Check what it picked up under **Learned instructions**, fix anything that's off, and click **Save learned instructions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the signature and the draft">
    Add your signature under **Your signature**, then open a real draft. Edit it and send it. Lindy picks up your edits over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
