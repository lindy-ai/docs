**Personal**: these settings apply to your own inbox.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, then Email drafting. Pick your inbox, and switch it on.
2. In Drafting instructions, say how you write. Short, friendly, plain language. No drafts for newsletters.
3. Lindy also learns how you write. Check what it picked up, fix anything that's off, and click Save learned instructions.
4. Add your signature, then open a real draft. Tweak it and send it. Lindy picks up your edits over time.
