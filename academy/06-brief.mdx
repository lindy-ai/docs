**Personal**: these settings apply to your own brief.

## Steps

1. Open Routines, find Daily briefs, switch it on, and open the card.
2. Tell it what to cover. Today's meetings, urgent client emails, and follow-ups. Keep it short.
3. Pick the calendars it should read, so your meetings make the cut.
4. Choose where it lands. Lindy chat is the easy start. Text and Slack work once they're set up.
5. Set the time, like seven thirty, and check your time zone.
6. Want a preview? Ask for a dry run, and see what your brief would include, without sending it.

   Example: `Dry-run my morning briefing so I can review what it would include, without delivering it.`
