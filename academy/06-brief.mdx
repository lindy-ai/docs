**Personal**: these settings apply to your own brief.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Daily briefs">
    Open **Routines**, find **Daily briefs**, switch it on, and open the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Lindy what to cover">
    In **Instructions**, say what to cover, like *today's meetings, urgent client emails, and follow-ups, kept short*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the calendar context">
    Pick the calendars it should read, so your meetings make the cut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where your brief arrives">
    Under **Delivery channel**, choose where it arrives. Lindy chat works right away. Email, text, and Slack DM work once they're set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your morning time">
    Set the time, like 7:30 AM. Your time zone is in **Settings**, under **General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview today's brief">
    Want a preview? Ask Lindy what today's brief would include, without sending it.

    For example: *"Show me what my daily brief would include today. Don't send it."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
