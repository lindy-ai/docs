**Personal or Team**: Only you keeps a connection personal. Owners and admins can share it with the workspace.

## Steps

1. **Open Integrations**: Open Integrations in the sidebar. These are the tools Lindy can work in for you.
2. **Find the tool you need**: Search for your tool and open it, like HubSpot or Notion.
3. **Connect the right account**: Choose Add account and follow the prompts. Check it's the right account before you approve.
4. **Know who can use it**: Under Availability, Only you keeps it personal. Admins can pick Your workspace to share it with the team.
5. **Try a simple read**: Then try it. Ask for something you'll recognize, like the Acme launch plan in Notion.
