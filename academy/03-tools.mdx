**Personal or Team**: **Only you** keeps a connection personal. Owners and admins can share their own connections with the workspace.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar. These are the tools Lindy can work in for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the tool you need">
    Search for your tool, like HubSpot or Notion, and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the right account">
    Click **Add account** and follow the prompts. Check that it's the right account before you approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know who can use it">
    Under **Availability**, **Only you** keeps the connection personal. Owners and admins can choose **Your workspace** to share it with the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a simple read">
    Ask for something you'll recognize, and tell Lindy not to change anything.

    For example: *"Find the Acme launch plan in Notion and list the next steps. Don't change anything."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
