**Personal or Team**: Only you keeps a connection personal. Owners and admins can share their own connections with the workspace.

## Steps

1. Open Integrations in the sidebar. These are the tools Lindy can work in for you.
2. Search for your tool and open it, like HubSpot or Notion.
3. Choose Add account and follow the prompts. Check it's the right account before you approve.
4. Under Availability, Only you keeps it personal. Owners and admins can pick Your workspace to share it with the team.
5. Then try it. Ask for something you'll recognize, like the Acme launch plan in Notion.

   Example: `Find the Acme launch plan in Notion and list the next steps. Don't change anything.`
