**Personal**: texting reaches your own Lindy, the one that knows your email, calendar, and meetings.

## Steps

1. **Find your phone settings**: Skipped your number at signup? Click your workspace menu, then Settings, then General. Add your phone, and enter the code.
2. **Find your personal Lindy**: Lindy texts you first. Save the contact, and pin the chat, so it's always one tap away.
3. **Start with one clear request**: Now text it the outcome you want, with a day and a time.
   Example: `Remind me Friday at 9am to follow up on the Acme proposal.`
4. **Send a voice memo**: Got a lot to hand off? Send a voice memo, like you would to a human assistant.
5. **Check the result**: And it's the same Lindy on the web. Ask what reminders are coming up, and check the timing.
   Example: `What reminders do I have coming up?`
