**Personal**: texting reaches your own Lindy, the one that knows your email, calendar, and meetings.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your phone settings">
    Skipped your number at signup? Open your workspace menu, then **Settings**, then **General**. Under **Phone number**, click **Add**, enter your number, and click **Continue**. Then text Lindy to verify: scan the QR code with your phone, or tap **Text Lindy to verify** on mobile. It verifies automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your personal Lindy">
    Lindy texts you back. Save the contact and pin the chat in Messages, so Lindy is always one tap away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with one clear request">
    Text it the outcome you want, with a day and a time.

    For example: *"Remind me Friday at 9am to follow up on the Acme proposal."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a voice memo">
    Got a lot to hand off? Send a voice memo instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    It's the same Lindy on the web. Ask what reminders are coming up, and check the timing.

    For example: *"What reminders do I have coming up?"*
  </Step>
</Steps>
