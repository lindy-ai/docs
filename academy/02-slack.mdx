**Team**: an admin installs Lindy once for the workspace, then each teammate links their own account.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Lindy for your team">
    An admin opens **Integrations** and clicks **Add to Slack**. This installs Lindy once for the whole workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Slack workspace">
    Slack asks for permission. Check that it's the right workspace, then click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the installation">
    Back in Lindy, Slack shows as connected. The team part is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your own Lindy account">
    Each teammate then links their own account: message Lindy in Slack, click **Connect Slack to Lindy**, and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your DM with Lindy">
    To find Lindy fast, press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), search for Lindy, and open the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first request">
    DM Lindy for your own work. For team work, invite it to a channel and @mention it.

    For example: *"Help me plan my priorities for today. Ask me what you need to know first."*
  </Step>
</Steps>
