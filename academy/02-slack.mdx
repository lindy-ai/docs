**Team**: an admin installs Lindy once for the workspace, then each teammate links their own account.

## Steps

1. No Lindy in your Slack yet? An admin opens Integrations and installs the Slack app, once for everyone.
2. Slack asks for permission. Check it's the right workspace, then hit Allow.
3. Back in Lindy, Slack shows as connected. The team part is done.
4. Now everyone else links their own account. Message Lindy, tap Connect Slack to Lindy, and confirm.
5. To find it fast, press Command K, or Control K on Windows. Search for Lindy, and open the app.
6. DM it for your own work. For team work, invite it to the channel, then mention it.

   Example: `Help me plan my priorities for today. Ask me what you need to know first.`
