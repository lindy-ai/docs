## Overview

Lindy joins your Microsoft Teams meetings, records them, and delivers a summary with decisions and action items afterwards.

With the Microsoft Teams integration, Lindy can:

- **Join your Teams meetings automatically** based on your connected calendar
- **Record and transcribe** the full conversation
- **Deliver summaries** with key decisions, action items, and highlights
- **Send follow-up emails** to attendees with a summary and next steps

Both Microsoft 365 (work and school) accounts and personal Microsoft accounts are supported.

## Prerequisites

You'll need:

- A [Lindy account](https://lindy.ai) (free or paid plan)
- A connected calendar (Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook) in Lindy
- Teams meetings with a join link on your calendar events

## Adding the Microsoft Teams Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Lindy">
    Go to [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai) and sign in to your Lindy account. If you don't have one yet, create a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    In your Lindy settings, connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook calendar. Lindy uses your calendar to detect upcoming Teams meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable meeting recording">
    In the settings panel, find **Meeting recording** and toggle it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which meetings to record">
    Select your recording scope:
    - **All meetings**: Lindy joins every meeting on your calendar
    - **External only**: record client and prospect calls, skip internal syncs
    - **Specific meetings**: choose exactly which meetings Lindy should join

    Lindy detects the Teams link on your calendar events and joins at the scheduled time. Nothing to configure on the Teams side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you only want Lindy to join certain meetings, start with **External only** or **Specific meetings** and expand from there.
</Tip>

## How Lindy Works with Microsoft Teams

Here's what happens for each meeting:

1. **Before the meeting**: Lindy detects the Teams link on your calendar event and prepares to join.
2. **During the meeting**: Lindy joins the call as **Lindy Meeting Notetaker**, a visible participant. It records and transcribes the conversation as it happens.
3. **After the meeting**: Lindy generates a structured summary and sends it to your inbox with a link to the full recording and transcript.

<Note>
  Lindy joins as a visible participant named "Lindy Meeting Notetaker" so everyone on the call knows it's being recorded.
</Note>

### Meeting Summaries

Each summary includes:

- **High-level summary**: the top bullet points covering key outcomes
- **Detailed notes**: in-depth coverage of the full discussion
- **Action items**: who needs to do what, with clear ownership
- **Full transcript**: searchable, timestamped transcript of the entire meeting
- **Recording link**: direct link to replay the meeting

You can customize the summary format, tone, and level of detail in your Lindy settings under **Summarization Instructions**.

### Searching Past Meetings

All meetings are saved to your Lindy dashboard. You can:

- Search across all meetings by keyword, person, or topic
- Ask Lindy about any past meeting in plain language (e.g., "What did we decide in last week's Teams call with Sarah?")
- Access full transcripts and recordings at any time

### Follow-Up Emails

Lindy can send follow-up emails after each meeting with a summary and action items. You choose who gets them:

- **All participants**: everyone on the invite
- **Internal only**: just your team
- **Specific people**: customize per your preferences

Learn more about [automatic follow-ups](/features/meeting-assistant/meeting-notes#post-meeting-follow-ups).

## What Data Lindy Accesses

Lindy's meeting bot joins as a participant and accesses only what is available during the call:

| Data | How it's used |
|---|---|
| **Meeting audio** | Transcription and summary generation |
| **Participant names** | Displayed in summaries and used for follow-ups |
| **Meeting metadata** | Title, time, and duration (from your calendar event) |

Lindy does **not** access:

- Your Microsoft Teams account, settings, or admin console
- Teams chat messages or channel conversations
- Meetings you have not opted in to record
- Screen shares or shared files
- Any Teams data outside of the meetings Lindy joins

All data is processed securely and stored in accordance with Lindy's [security policies](/resources/security).

## Removing the Integration

You can stop Lindy from joining your Teams meetings at any time:

- **Turn off meeting recording**: Go to your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai), find **Meeting recording**, and toggle it off. Lindy stops joining new meetings immediately.
- **Tell Lindy to stop**: You can also just tell Lindy to stop joining specific meetings or all meetings.

### What happens to your data

- Existing recordings, transcripts, and summaries stay in your Lindy dashboard
- You can delete individual meetings or your entire history from the dashboard at any time
- Once recording is off, Lindy stops collecting any new data from your Teams meetings

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lindy can't join a meeting that requires all participants to be signed in">
    Some Microsoft Teams meetings are configured so every participant must be signed in to a Microsoft account. Lindy's notetaker joins as an anonymous guest and cannot satisfy this requirement.

    To work around it, ask the organizer to:
    - Allow guest or anonymous joiners on the meeting, or
    - Admit Lindy manually from the lobby when it tries to join

    This is a Microsoft Teams policy, not a Lindy limitation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lindy joined but was dropped mid-meeting">
    Teams occasionally drops guest participants due to network or admission changes on the host side. Lindy will still generate a summary from whatever portion of the meeting it was able to record before being dropped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Lindy work with both Teams for work and personal Teams accounts?">
    Yes. Lindy supports Microsoft Teams meetings from both Microsoft 365 (work and school) and personal Microsoft accounts. The following meeting link formats are supported:

    - `teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/...` (Teams for work and school)
    - `teams.microsoft.com/meet/...` (Meet now and shareable links)
    - `teams.live.com/meet/...` (personal Microsoft accounts)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the recording visible to other participants?">
    Yes. Lindy joins as a participant named "Lindy Meeting Notetaker" so all attendees are aware the meeting is being recorded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Lindy work with Zoom and Google Meet too?">
    Yes. Lindy supports Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet. The same meeting recording settings apply to all three platforms. See the [Zoom integration](/skills/popular-integrations/zoom) or the [Meeting Notes](/features/meeting-assistant/meeting-notes) overview for more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
