## Overview

Lindy integrates with Zoom to automatically join your meetings, record them, and deliver structured summaries with action items. No manual note-taking required.

With the Zoom integration, Lindy can:

- **Join your Zoom meetings automatically** based on your connected calendar
- **Record and transcribe** the full conversation in real time
- **Generate AI summaries** with key decisions, action items, and highlights
- **Send follow-up emails** to attendees with a summary and next steps

## Prerequisites

Before using Lindy with Zoom, make sure you have:

- A [Lindy account](https://app.lindy.ai) (free or paid plan)
- A connected calendar (Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook) in Lindy
- Zoom meetings with a join link on your calendar events

## Adding the Zoom Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Lindy">
    Go to [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai) and sign in to your Lindy account. If you don't have one yet, create a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    In your Lindy settings, connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook calendar. Lindy uses your calendar to detect upcoming Zoom meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable meeting recording">
    In the settings panel, find **Meeting recording** and toggle it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which meetings to record">
    Select your recording scope:
    - **All meetings**: Lindy joins every meeting on your calendar
    - **External only**: record client and prospect calls, skip internal syncs
    - **Specific meetings**: choose exactly which meetings Lindy should join

    Lindy automatically detects the Zoom link on your calendar events and joins at the scheduled time. No additional Zoom-side configuration is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you only want Lindy to join certain meetings, start with **External only** or **Specific meetings** and expand from there.
</Tip>

## How Lindy Works with Zoom

Once enabled, Lindy handles everything automatically:

1. **Before the meeting**: Lindy detects the Zoom link on your calendar event and prepares to join.
2. **During the meeting**: Lindy joins the call as **Lindy Meeting Notetaker**, a visible participant. It records audio and transcribes the conversation in real time.
3. **After the meeting**: Lindy generates a structured summary and sends it to your inbox with a link to the full recording and transcript.

<Note>
  Lindy joins as a visible participant named "Lindy Meeting Notetaker" so all attendees are aware the meeting is being recorded. This supports recording consent best practices.
</Note>

### Meeting Summaries

Lindy's AI-powered summaries include:

- **High-level summary**: the top bullet points covering key outcomes
- **Detailed notes**: in-depth coverage of the full discussion
- **Action items**: who needs to do what, with clear ownership
- **Full transcript**: searchable, timestamped transcript of the entire meeting
- **Recording link**: direct link to replay the meeting

You can customize the summary format, tone, and level of detail in your Lindy settings under **Summarization Instructions**.

### Searching Past Meetings

All meetings are saved to your Lindy dashboard. You can:

- Search across all meetings by keyword, person, or topic
- Ask Lindy about any past meeting in plain language (e.g., "What did we decide in last week's Zoom with Sarah?")
- Access full transcripts and recordings at any time

### Follow-Up Emails

Lindy can automatically send follow-up emails after each meeting with a summary and action items. You can configure this to:

- Send to all attendees
- Send only to yourself
- Disable follow-ups entirely

Learn more about [automatic follow-ups](/features/meeting-assistant/follow-ups).

## What Data Lindy Accesses

Lindy's meeting bot joins as a participant and accesses only what is available during the call:

| Data | How it's used |
|---|---|
| **Meeting audio** | Transcription and summary generation |
| **Participant names** | Displayed in summaries and used for follow-ups |
| **Meeting metadata** | Title, time, and duration (from your calendar event) |

Lindy does **not** access:

- Your Zoom account, settings, or billing information
- Zoom chat messages
- Meetings you have not opted in to record
- Screen shares or shared files
- Any Zoom data outside of the meetings Lindy joins

All data is processed securely and stored in accordance with Lindy's [security policies](/resources/security).

## Removing the Integration

You can stop Lindy from joining your Zoom meetings at any time.

### Disable meeting recording

1. Go to your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai)
2. Find **Meeting recording** in the settings panel
3. Toggle it off

Lindy will immediately stop joining new meetings.

### Remove from Zoom Marketplace

To fully remove Lindy from your Zoom account:

1. Sign in to [zoom.us](https://zoom.us)
2. Go to the **Zoom App Marketplace**
3. Click **Manage** > **Installed Apps**
4. Find **Lindy** and click **Uninstall**

### What happens to your data

- Existing meeting recordings, transcripts, and summaries remain in your Lindy dashboard
- You can delete individual meetings or your entire meeting history from the dashboard at any time
- After removal, Lindy stops collecting any new data from your Zoom meetings
- No residual background processes or data collection continue after uninstall

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lindy didn't join my Zoom meeting">
    - Confirm your calendar is connected in Lindy settings
    - Check that meeting recording is toggled on
    - Verify the calendar event contains a valid Zoom meeting link (e.g., `https://zoom.us/j/...`)
    - If using **Specific meetings** mode, confirm you selected this meeting for recording
    - Make sure the meeting wasn't cancelled or rescheduled after Lindy checked your calendar
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see a summary after the meeting">
    - Summaries are typically delivered within a few minutes after the meeting ends
    - Check your email inbox, including spam and promotions folders
    - For longer meetings (90+ minutes), processing may take slightly longer
    - Verify that Lindy successfully joined the meeting (check your Lindy dashboard)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Participants see 'Lindy Meeting Notetaker' joining">
    This is expected behavior. Lindy joins as a visible participant so all attendees know the meeting is being recorded. This ensures transparency and supports recording consent requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recording quality issues">
    - Lindy records audio from the Zoom call directly, so audio quality depends on participants' connections
    - If transcription seems inaccurate, check that participants have clear audio and minimal background noise
    - Summaries are generated from the transcript, so clearer audio produces better results
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid Zoom plan?">
    No. Lindy works with any Zoom plan, including the free tier. As long as your calendar event has a valid Zoom meeting link, Lindy can join.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I record only specific meetings?">
    Yes. In your Lindy settings, choose **Specific meetings** under the recording scope to select exactly which meetings Lindy should join.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the recording visible to other participants?">
    Yes. Lindy joins as a participant named "Lindy Meeting Notetaker" so all attendees are aware the meeting is being recorded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long are recordings stored?">
    Recordings and transcripts are stored in your Lindy dashboard for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual meetings at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Lindy work with Google Meet too?">
    Yes. Lindy supports both Zoom and Google Meet. The same meeting recording settings apply to both platforms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I customize what the summary includes?">
    Yes. Go to your Lindy settings and edit the **Summarization Instructions** to adjust the format, tone, and level of detail.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
