Lindy connects to your existing tools so she can take action across your stack — reading emails, updating your CRM, posting to Slack, and more.

## Connecting an Integration

1. Go to [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai) and open **Settings**
2. Click **Integrations**
3. Find the tool you want to connect and click **Connect**
4. Follow the prompts to authenticate
