## Add Lindy to Your Slack Workspace

Get started in minutes — no code required.

<Note>
**Where to find the "Add to Slack" button:** The Slack connection is available inside your Lindy dashboard after creating a free account. Follow the steps below to access it.
</Note>

### How to Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Free Lindy Account">
    Go to [chat.lindy.ai/signup](https://chat.lindy.ai/signup) and sign up. No credit card required.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Open the Integrations Page">
    Once logged in, click **Settings** (gear icon) in the left sidebar, then select **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Slack'">
    Find Slack in the integrations list and click the **Connect** button. This will open Slack's authorization page.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Authorize Lindy">
    Review the permissions and click **Allow** to connect your Slack workspace to Lindy.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Configure Your Bot">
    Choose which channels to monitor, set up trigger phrases, and connect your knowledge base.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Use Cases

### Internal Knowledge Bot
Answer employee questions about policies, procedures, and company information using your HR documents, handbooks, and wikis.

### Customer Support Assistant
Respond to customer inquiries in shared Slack channels, pulling information from your help docs and FAQ.

### Sales Alert System
Get instant notifications when new leads arrive, deals close, or important CRM events occur.

### Meeting Notes Distribution
Automatically share meeting summaries and action items to relevant channels after calls end.
