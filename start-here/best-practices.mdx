<Note>
**Welcome to saving 2 hours a day!** Share as much about yourself with Lindy as possible. The more she knows, the less she has to ask.
</Note>

- **Pin Lindy as a favorite in iMessage** so she's always one tap away
- **Start with a voice note** — avoid writer's block by brain-dumping for 5 minutes like you're briefing a new hire
- **Say more than you think you should** — the more Lindy knows, the less she has to ask
- **Give lots of feedback** — phrases like "never do this" or "always remember this" help Lindy learn fast
- **Start small, build from there** — pick one daily time sink and let Lindy own it, then keep adding

## Before You Begin

Open iMessage, hit record, and talk through the items below like you're briefing a new hire.

**On mobile?** Start with just the basics under "Start here." You can send multiple voice notes over the next few days. Let Lindy know you'll be sending a few so she keeps building on what you've already shared.

**On desktop?** Open iMessage on your phone and go through as many sections as you want in one sitting. Five minutes covers a lot.

You don't need to do everything on this list. The basics are enough to get Lindy working for you today.

## Start Here

Record a voice note covering these four things. That's all Lindy needs to get useful on day one.

1. Your name, role, and company
2. What you spend most of your day doing
3. Things eating too much of your time
4. Your work hours and time zone

Everything below is optional context you can share whenever you want, in any order.

## Daily Basics

### Calendar and Schedule

- Days or blocks you protect (no-meeting Fridays, deep work mornings, etc.)
- Default meeting length you prefer
- Buffer time between meetings
- Preferred meeting windows

### Meetings

- Which meetings Lindy should join or skip (external, internal, 1:1s, etc.)
- Post-meeting tasks that eat your time (follow-up emails, CRM updates, sending notes on Slack, etc.)

### Email

- Your most important contacts
- The kinds of emails you want Lindy to alert you about
- The kinds of emails to auto-archive

### Daily Briefings

- Whether you prefer text summaries or voice note recaps
- The time you wake up and would like to receive daily briefs
- Any content to include (motivational quotes, news, market updates, etc.)
- Whether you'd also like an end-of-day brief

## All About You

### Priorities and Goals

- Your top priorities right now
- Projects deserving the most calendar space
- What you wish you had more time for
- KPIs or goals you're tracking

### Personal Life

- Names of partner, kids, and key family members
- Family events and recurring obligations (anniversaries, school pickups, date nights, etc.)
- Favorite restaurants and neighborhoods
- Dietary restrictions or preferences
- Go-to gift categories (books, experiences, wine, etc.)

### How You Want to Feel

- What a successful day looks like for you
- Whether Lindy should stay positive and motivating, or give direct unfiltered feedback
- Whether Lindy should flag when you're overloaded and suggest changes
- Personal routines Lindy should support (morning rituals, exercise, meditation, etc.)

### Energy and Focus

- Tasks that drain you most
- Times of day when you do your best thinking
- Recurring commitments that should never be moved

## Going Deeper

### Tools You Already Use

- Your current productivity stack (project management, CRM, notes, spreadsheets, AI tools)
- Your sources of truth for tasks, clients, or projects
- Key documents or sheets you reference regularly

### Tasks and Accountability

- Where your tasks live right now (your head, sticky notes, an app, etc.)
- Whether you want Lindy to maintain a running task list and where to store it
- How aggressive Lindy should be with reminders
- Monthly, quarterly, or annual recurring reminders (gifts, reviews, invoices, renewals)

## Ask Lindy To...

Here are prompts you can send Lindy right now via iMessage or [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai).

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Calendar and scheduling">
- "Block 2 hours for deep work this week"
- "Move my 3pm to Thursday"
- "What does my week look like?"
- "Am I free Friday afternoon?"
- "Schedule a 30-min call with [name] next week"
- "Cancel my meeting with [name] tomorrow"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Email">
- "Draft a reply to that last email from [name]"
- "Follow up with [name] if they don't reply by Wednesday"
- "Send [name] a thank you note for today's call"
- "What emails need my attention right now?"
- "Draft a cold intro to [name] at [company]"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Meeting prep and follow-up">
- "Prep me for my next call"
- "Research [person/company] before my 2pm"
- "Send [name] a recap of our call with next steps"
- "What action items came out of today's meetings?"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reminders and follow-ups">
- "Remind me to follow up with [name] on Thursday"
- "Remind me every Monday to review my pipeline"
- "Remind me about [name]'s birthday next month"
- "Check in with me at 5pm about whether I finished the proposal"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Research and information">
- "Summarize this" + paste a link or article
- "What's [company] been in the news for lately?"
- "Find me a good restaurant near [location] for a client dinner"
- "Compare [tool A] vs [tool B] for project management"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Daily habits">
- "Send me my wins from the day at 8pm, every day"
- "Give me a positive quote every morning at 7am"
- "Text me my top 3 priorities when I wake up"
- "Recap my day at 9pm as a voice note"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Tasks and accountability">
- "Add [task] to my to-do list"
- "What's still on my list from this week?"
- "What did I say I'd do after yesterday's meetings?"
- "Remind me about this in 2 hours"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Personal">
- "Order flowers for [partner] for our anniversary on the 15th"
- "Find a gift under $100 for [name]"
- "Book a reservation for 2 at [restaurant] Saturday night"
- "What's coming up this week that I might forget about?"
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
