Add Lindy to your team's Slack, connect your tools, and @mention it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it to Slack">
    Install Lindy from the [Slack marketplace](https://lindy.ai). One click, and the whole team gets Lindy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Authorize the tools you already use. Lindy sorts out the permissions for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="@mention it">
    Type `@Lindy` in any channel and ask. It starts working right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
