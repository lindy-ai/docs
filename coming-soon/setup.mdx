Setting up Lindy has two parts: an admin adds Lindy to your Slack workspace, then each person links their account and connects their tools.

## 1. Set up your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Lindy into Slack">
    From Lindy (signed in), open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find **Slack** under Popular.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/slack-find-integration.png" alt="The Integrations page in Lindy, with arrows pointing to the Integrations item in the sidebar and to the Slack card under Popular" />
    </Frame>

    Open the Slack integration and click **Add account**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/slack-add-account.png" alt="The Slack integration page in Lindy with the Add account button above the list of Slack actions" />
    </Frame>

    Slack's standard approval screen opens so you can approve the bot's permissions. Pick your workspace, then click **Allow**. As the installer, you're linked to your Lindy account right away, with no extra step.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/slack-approve-permissions.png" alt="Slack's Review app permissions screen with an arrow pointing to the Allow button" />
    </Frame>

    Right after install, Lindy sends you a welcome DM confirming it's connected and explaining how to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teammates link with one tap">
    Everyone else links themselves. The first time a teammate DMs or @mentions Lindy, it doesn't start working yet, it sends one DM with a **Connect Slack to Lindy** button. They tap it, confirm while signed into Lindy, and their Slack identity is bound to their Lindy account. The message they sent is replayed automatically once they're linked, so nothing is lost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Set up your Lindy

Once you're linked, connect your own tools so Lindy can work for you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect email and calendar">
    Connect your Google or Microsoft account so Lindy can run your inbox and schedule meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your phone">
    Add your phone number (or connect Apple Messages) so you can text Lindy. You'll verify with a code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to talk to Lindy

- **DM the bot** to start or continue your own rolling task. Replies come back in the DM.
- **@mention Lindy in a channel or thread** and it replies inside that thread, never as a new top-level message. Invite the bot to the channel first if it isn't there. Once it's replied, a clear follow-up can continue the thread without re-@mentioning.
- **Slack's AI sidebar** works too; replies stay in that sidebar thread.
- **Auto-join:** the bot can join public channels when linked teammates join, and leave when the last one does. This is configurable.

## What Lindy can do

- **In shared channels and threads:** web search, image generation, public Slack search, and Notion (if your team has connected it). Lindy keeps answers public-safe: it summarizes rather than pasting private data into a public channel.
- **In your DMs and your own threads:** the full toolset, including calendar scheduling, email, SMS, reminders, and meeting reads.
- **Extras:** private summaries when you're pulled into a long thread, and follow-up nudges for asks that haven't gotten a reply.

## Guardrails and approvals

Each connected account has one setting: **Always allow** or **Ask for approval**, applied to every action that writes or sends. Read-only actions never need approval. Approval is enforced in shared Slack threads; your personal DMs keep their existing confirmation behavior.
