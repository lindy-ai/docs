Lindy has a real file system that you and your assistant share. When Lindy builds you a spreadsheet, a report, or a PDF, it lands here. Open it, edit it, and Lindy sees your changes the next time it works. Every version is kept, so you can always go back.

## How to Set It Up

<Note>
  Find **Files** (the folder icon) in the left sidebar. There's nothing to turn on.
</Note>

## Personal and team files

Files come in two areas:

| Area | Who can see it | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | Just you | Just you |
| **Team** | Everyone in your workspace | Workspace admins |

If you're not an admin, the Team area is read-only, and Lindy shows a banner to say so.

## Where files come from

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your assistant makes them">
    Ask Lindy to build a spreadsheet, a doc, a PDF, or a report. It does the work and saves the result to your Files, ready to open.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="You make them">
    Use **New file** or **New folder** to create your own. Edit right in the browser, rename, drag to move, or download.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  You can't upload files from your computer yet. For now, files come from your assistant or from what you create in the browser. Downloads work fine.
</Note>

## Working with your assistant

Ask Lindy the way you always do. Behind the scenes it reads and writes your files, runs code, and turns out spreadsheets, PDFs, and reports. The results show up in your Files on their own.

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every change is saved">
    Lindy keeps a full history of each file. Compare versions and roll back any time. Deleting a file keeps its history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edit at the same time, no conflicts">
    If you and Lindy edit the same file at once, you get a clean merge view instead of a mess of conflict markers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Size limits">
    Files up to about 1MB are editable in the browser. Larger files are view and download only, up to about 200MB.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
