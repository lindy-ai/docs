Home is the first thing you see when you open Lindy. A big **Ask Lindy anything…** box sits front and center, and around it a set of cards shows what Lindy has learned, what's coming up, what needs your attention, and what it's been up to. You drive Lindy from the box. The cards are Lindy reporting back.

## Ask Lindy anything

The composer at the top is how you work. Type a request and Lindy takes it on as a task and opens it for you. Try:

- "Prep me for my next meeting"
- "Draft a reply to Sarah's email"
- "What's on my calendar today?"

Not sure where to start? The **What Lindy can do for you** tiles fill the box with a ready-made request. Edit it, then send.

## Finish your setup first

The **Complete your setup** checklist gets your accounts connected so the rest of Home has something to show:

1. Connect Google or Microsoft for email and calendar
2. Share your phone number so you can text Lindy
3. Add any other email accounts you want Lindy to run
4. Invite your team

Most cards stay quiet until your accounts are connected, so run this first.

## What's on the page

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upcoming meetings" icon="calendar">
    Your next calendar events, with a **Join** button for anything that has a link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Past meetings" icon="circle-play">
    Recent meetings with recaps. Click one to open its recap, or **See all** for your library.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Need to know" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Things Lindy flagged from email, meetings, and Slack, each linking back to the source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What I've been up to" icon="chart-simple">
    Emails triaged, drafts written, and time saved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent learnings" icon="lightbulb">
    The preferences Lindy has picked up about you. Edit them inline to correct or add a fact.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How to reach me" icon="at">
    Lindy's email and phone number, so you can reach it by text or email outside the app too.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Hit **Customize** in the header to turn any card on or off, so Home shows only what you care about.
</Tip>

## Getting started, in order

1. Run through **Complete your setup**: accounts, phone, team.
2. Click a **What Lindy can do** tile if you're not sure what to ask.
3. Reach Lindy by email or text too, from **How to reach me**.
4. Keep **Recent learnings** accurate so Lindy works the way you want.
5. Use **Customize** to hide cards you don't need.
