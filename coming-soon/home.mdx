Home isn't a dashboard of buttons to configure. It's where you talk to Lindy and see what it's been doing for you. Everything on the page is built around that.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/home-page.png" alt="The Home page: a greeting, the chat box with an @ hint, suggestion tiles, past meetings, and Need to know and What I've been up to cards" />
</Frame>

## Talk to Lindy

The box at the top is your **personal Lindy**, connected to your own integrations like email, calendar, and the other tools you've linked. Ask it anything and it takes the request on as a task.

Type `@` to point Lindy at a specific meeting, folder, or tool. Around the box, a set of cards shows what Lindy has been up to and what needs your attention.

## Your activity and context cards

As Lindy starts working, these fill in. Empty cards stay hidden or show a friendly "getting to know you" state until there's data.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upcoming meetings" icon="calendar">
    Today's meetings, with a **Join** button for video calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Past meetings" icon="circle-play">
    Recorded meetings with recaps. Until Lindy joins one, this stays empty.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Need to know" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Top items pulled from email, Slack, and meetings that you should act on. Clickable, and dismissable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What I've been up to" icon="chart-simple">
    Stats like emails triaged, drafts written, and estimated time saved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent learnings" icon="lightbulb">
    Things Lindy has learned about you. Add, edit, or delete any of them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How to reach me" icon="at">
    Lindy's email (lindy@lindy.ai) and your assigned Lindy phone number, ready to copy.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The sidebar

On the left: **Home, Chat, Meetings, Files, Routines, Skills, and Integrations** (which ones show depends on your setup), plus search, a **New chat** button, the workspace switcher, and your credit balance at the bottom.

## Make it yours

Use the **Customize** menu ("Show on home") to show or hide cards, and dismiss any card with its **X**. Your layout preference syncs across devices, including the Chrome extension's new-tab page.

<Tip>
  New here? Finish the setup checklist (connect email and phone), then start typing in the chat box or click a suggestion tile. Everything else on the page fills itself in as Lindy works.
</Tip>
