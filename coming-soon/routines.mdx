Routines are recurring tasks you set up once and Lindy runs on its own. You tell Lindy what to do and when, and it handles the rest. They are what make Lindy proactive instead of only reactive.

<Note>
  Open **Routines** from the left sidebar to see every routine you have, whether it's on, and a summary of what each one does.
</Note>

## Personal vs team routines

Every routine has a scope:

- **Personal routines**: just for you. Only you see them and only you manage them. Create and edit them from chat or the Routines page.
- **Team routines**: shared across the whole workspace. Managed from the Routines page in settings, not from chat.

## Find your routines

You can see your routines in two places:

- **The Routines page**: a dedicated section in your settings. It lists every routine, its on or off state, and what it does.
- **In chat**: ask Lindy *"what routines do I have?"* or *"list my routines"* and it shows you.

## Create a routine

From chat, tell Lindy what you want in plain language:

- *"Every morning at 8am, send me a summary of my calendar."*
- *"Every Monday at 9am, check my inbox for emails from investors and alert me."*
- *"When I get an email from a specific person, forward it to my team."*

Lindy handles the setup. There is nothing technical to configure. Be clear about three things:

- **When it runs**: daily, weekly, or a specific time.
- **What it does**: the task itself.
- **Where the result goes**: SMS, Slack DM, email, and so on.

## Edit a routine

To change a routine, just ask:

- *"Change my morning routine to 9am instead of 8am."*
- *"Rename my standup reminder to Team Standup."*
- *"Update my daily briefing to include weather."*

You can change the name, the instructions, or the schedule. You cannot switch the kind of trigger from chat, like turning a timed routine into an event-based one. That needs the Routines page.

## Manage routines

- **Pause or resume**: *"pause my morning routine"* or *"turn it back on."* This stops it from firing without deleting it.
- **Delete**: *"delete my morning routine."* This is permanent, so you'll need to recreate it to get it back.
- **Check status**: *"what routines are active?"* shows everything with its on or off state.

## How it works

When you create a routine, Lindy sets up a trigger with your instructions attached. The trigger is either timed (*"every Monday at 9am"*) or event-based (*"when an email arrives"*). When it fires, Lindy follows your instructions: it can search your email, check your calendar, send messages, update Notion, or whatever else you asked for.

The instructions are the most important part. The more specific you are about what to do and how, the better the result. Vague instructions produce vague results.

## Routines vs built-in features

Routines are different from Lindy's built-in features. Your daily brief, meeting prep, meeting recording, and email triage are separate settings you configure on their own. Routines are for **custom** recurring tasks that don't have a built-in equivalent. If you set up a routine that duplicates a built-in feature, Lindy points you to the setting instead.
