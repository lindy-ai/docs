A routine is a standing instruction your assistant runs on its own. Set the schedule and the prompt once, and Lindy handles the rest: every weekday at 9, every Monday morning, the first of the month. Each run lands in the routine's history, so you can see exactly what happened.

## How to Set It Up

<Note>
  Find your routines in the left sidebar under **Routines**. Toggle between your **Personal** routines and your team's at the top of the page.
</Note>

## The routines page

## Create a routine

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On the Routines page">
    Click **New routine**. You've got two ways to set it up:

    - **Describe it**: write it in plain language, like *"Every weekday at 9, summarize my unread email and Slack mentions."* Lindy reads that and fills in the name and schedule for you.
    - **Set it up yourself**: pick the **name**, the **schedule** (daily, weekly, or monthly, plus a time), and the **prompt**.

    Click **Create routine**. New routines start on.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In chat">
    Tell your assistant what to do and when, and it makes the routine for you. It shows up under **Custom**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Manage a routine

Open any custom routine to edit its name, schedule, and prompt, or to see its history. Each card shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **On** | Running on schedule |
| **Paused** | Off for now, won't run until you turn it back on |
| **Connect account** | Needs a connected account first |
| **Needs setup** | Missing a step before it can run |

To **pause** a routine, flip its toggle. To **delete** one, open it and click **Delete**. Open any routine to see its recent runs, each with a link to the task it kicked off.

## Good to know

- Routines run on a **schedule** today. Triggers that fire on an event, like a new email landing, are on the way.
- **Team routines** are created and edited by workspace admins. Everyone else can see them.
- A routine needs its connected accounts and a way to reach you. Until it has both, it shows **Needs setup** instead of turning on.
