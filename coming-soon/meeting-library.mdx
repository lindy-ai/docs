Meeting Folders live in your meeting library. Group related meetings into a folder, then share the whole folder with a teammate or your whole workspace. Everyone you share with sees every meeting inside.

## How to Set It Up

<Note>
  Open your meetings from the left sidebar. You'll find **Meeting Folders** there, with a **+** to create one.
</Note>

## Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + to create a folder">
    Find **Meeting Folders** in the left sidebar and click **+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set who can see it">
    Give the folder a name and choose its visibility. If you pick **Specific people**, add the teammates who should have access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-file meetings (optional)">
    Turn on **Automatically add meetings** and describe what belongs here, like *"any sales call."* Lindy files matching meetings as they finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing a folder

You share a folder by setting who can see it. There's no separate share button:

| Visibility | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Only you** | Just you |
| **Specific people** | Only the teammates you add |
| **Your entire team** | Everyone in your workspace |

Anyone you add gets a notification. Change the visibility later and Lindy re-shares it.

## Add and remove meetings

On any meeting, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add or remove from folders**, then pick the folders you want. Inside a folder, the same menu takes a meeting back out.

<Tip>
  With auto-filing on, Lindy sorts new meetings into the right folder as soon as they wrap. Internal-only meetings are left out.
</Tip>

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner or admin** | Rename, change who can see it, and delete. Deleting a folder keeps the meetings, it just removes the grouping. |
| **Everyone you share with** | Open the folder and its meetings, and add or remove meetings. |

## Sharing a single meeting

Want to send just one meeting, not a whole folder? On a meeting page, use **Share to attendees** to email the recap: the meeting on its own, with a summary, or with a custom summary and a note. People outside Lindy open it from a link.
