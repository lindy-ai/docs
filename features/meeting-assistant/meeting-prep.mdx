## Never Walk Into a Meeting Blind

Delegate meeting prep to Lindy. Before every call, a briefing lands in your inbox or on your phone with who you're meeting, what you've discussed before, and what's on the agenda. You don't have to ask.

## How to Set It Up

Meeting prep is a built-in **routine**. On the **Routines** page, open the **Personal** tab, find the **Meeting prep** card ("Gives context on your meetings"), and toggle it on. Click the card to choose which meetings get prep, when you receive it, and its format.

## What's Included

| Section | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Time** | When the meeting is scheduled |
| **Meeting name** | The title and subject |
| **Attendees** | List of all participants |
| **Attendee context** | For external attendees: job title, current company, and LinkedIn profile |
| **Past meetings** | Summary of previous meetings with the same people (if recurring) |

You can customize the prep format in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Meeting prep** in the settings panel. Describe your preferred structure, tone, and level of detail in the text field, and Lindy follows your instructions.

## Which Meetings Get Prep

- **All meetings**: every event on your calendar
- **External meetings only**: skip internal team syncs, focus on client and prospect meetings

- **Daily digest**: receive all meeting prep at a set time each day (e.g., 5:00 AM)
- **Before each meeting**: receive prep a set number of minutes before the meeting starts (e.g., 15 minutes before)

You can enable one or both.

## How You Receive Prep

- **Email**: lands in your inbox
- **SMS / iMessage**: sent directly to your phone

<Note>
  To change how you're notified, go to the **Routines** page, open the **Personal** tab, and click the **Meeting prep** card. Pick where prep lands in the **Delivery channel** field.
</Note>
