## Never Walk Into a Meeting Blind

<Frame caption="TODO: Screenshot of Meeting Prep settings panel">
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/what-is-lindy-banner.jpg" alt="Placeholder" />
</Frame>

Lindy automatically prepares a briefing before every meeting. You don't have to ask. You get context on who you're meeting with, what you've discussed before, and what's on the agenda, delivered to your inbox or phone before the meeting starts.

## What's Included

Lindy's meeting prep covers:

| Section | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Time** | When the meeting is scheduled |
| **Meeting name** | The title and subject |
| **Attendees** | List of all participants |
| **Attendee context** | For external attendees: job title, current company, and LinkedIn profile |
| **Past meetings** | Summary of previous meetings with the same people (if recurring) |

You can customize the prep format in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Meeting prep** in the settings panel. Tell Lindy what to include, the structure, tone, and level of detail you prefer.

## Which Meetings Get Prep

Choose which meetings Lindy preps for:

- **All meetings**: every event on your calendar
- **External meetings only**: skip internal team syncs, focus on client and prospect meetings

## When You Receive Prep

Two timing options:

- **Daily digest**: receive all meeting prep at a set time each day (e.g., 5:00 AM)
- **Before each meeting**: receive prep a set number of minutes before the meeting starts (e.g., 15 minutes before)

<Frame caption="TODO: Screenshot of prep delivery timing settings">

</Frame>

You can enable one or both.

## How You Receive Prep

Choose how Lindy delivers your briefing:

- **Email**: lands in your inbox
- **SMS / iMessage**: sent directly to your phone

<Tip>
Getting meeting prep via text is great when you're on the go. You can glance at the briefing on your phone before walking into a conference room.
</Tip>
