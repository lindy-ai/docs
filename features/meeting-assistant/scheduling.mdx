## Set Your Availability

For scheduling by email or chat, set your preferences in plain language: preferred hours, buffers between meetings, blocked times, default meeting length, and meeting types. You can update them anytime.

Booking links can use your usual hours or custom hours for each link. Set these in the link's [Availability tab](/features/booking-links#adjust-availability-and-settings).

<Note>
  Meeting scheduling is a built-in **routine**. On the **Routines** page, open the **Personal** tab and click the **Meeting scheduling** card to turn it on and set your hours, buffers, and calendar rules.
</Note>

## 1. CC-Based Scheduling (Coming Soon)

CC your Lindy assistant's email address on any thread where you're trying to find time, and Lindy takes over as your scheduling assistant. It reads the thread, checks your calendar, and handles the rest: no questions, no back-and-forth.

</div>

<Steps>
  <Step title="CC Lindy on a scheduling thread">
    Add your Lindy email address to the **CC line** of an email where you and the other person are trying to find a time. The CC is the signal for Lindy to step in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy reads the thread and takes over">
    Lindy pulls the details from the thread (duration, date range, time of day, attendees, timezone) and fills any gaps with sensible defaults. If you've already agreed on a time, it books it. If not, it replies once with 3 open slots as clickable links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The meeting gets booked">
    The meeting books automatically on your calendar with invites sent, exactly like Smart Scheduling above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Two ways it plays out

**When the time's already agreed**, Lindy books it. **When you still need a time**, Lindy proposes three slots. Either way, the Cc is all it takes.

<Note>
Lindy only acts when **both** are true: your Lindy email is on the CC line, **and** the thread shows scheduling intent ("let's find time", "can we grab 30 min?"). CC without a scheduling ask, or a scheduling ask without the CC, won't trigger it.
</Note>

<Tip>
Want to constrain the options? Just say so in the thread ("mornings only", "next week", "30 min"). Otherwise Lindy uses your standard meeting length, working hours, and buffers.
</Tip>

## 2. Email Drafts

When someone emails you about scheduling a meeting, Lindy detects the request and offers times in a draft reply on your behalf. Your recipient picks one in a single click and the meeting drops onto both calendars.

- When someone emails to schedule, Lindy offers times in a draft reply
- Recipients book a slot in one click, with no back-and-forth
- Save ~3 minutes per invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Someone emails you asking for a time to meet">
    Lindy detects the scheduling request and takes over, with no action needed from you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy drafts a reply with 3 available times">
    Based on your preferred hours, buffer time, meeting length, and calendar availability, Lindy drafts a reply with three open slots as clickable links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They click a time and it's instantly booked">
    When the other person clicks a link, the meeting is automatically added to both calendars. No back-and-forth, no scheduling tools, no manual entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Ad Hoc Scheduling

When you want Lindy to handle scheduling on your behalf, just ask. Text or message it and it finds the time, reaches out, and locks it in.

### Add or Update Events on the Fly

Use Lindy to add events to your calendar or change them with one line of text:

- "Add a meeting location"
- "Pick a restaurant for the client dinner"
- "Swap the venue and notify attendees"
- "Move my 2pm to Thursday"
- "Cancel my call with [name]"

Send Lindy a screenshot of a text conversation and it pulls the meeting details onto the right day.

### End-to-End Outreach

Or send Lindy on a mission and let it run the whole loop:

> "Find time with Bob on Saturday"

It reaches out, proposes times, locks the meeting in, and adds it to your calendar.

### Recurring Routines

Set up recurring workflows with one text:

> "Every week, audit my calendar and make sure I have a 45-min walk break at least once a day"

### Internal Calendars

Lindy can check any teammate's calendar that's shared with your workspace, so you can ask it to find time between people on your team without any back-and-forth.

### How to Delegate

- **iMessage / SMS**: "Book a call with Sarah next Tuesday afternoon"
- **Web app**: chat at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai)

<Tip>
Scheduling over text is the fastest option. Lindy already knows your calendar and preferences, so a simple text is all it takes.
</Tip>
