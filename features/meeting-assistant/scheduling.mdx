You say who and when. Lindy finds the time, sends the invite, and puts it on your calendar. No scheduling links, no back-and-forth emails.

## How It Works

- **"Schedule a meeting with [name] next week"**: Lindy finds open slots on your calendar and shares available times with the other person
- **"Find 30 minutes for a call with [name] this Thursday"**: Lindy checks your availability and sends the invite
- **"Schedule a meeting with a screenshot"**: send a screenshot of someone's availability and Lindy matches it against your calendar

## Rescheduling & Changes

Plans change. Text Lindy "Move my 2pm to Thursday" or "Cancel my call with [name]" and it handles the update, notifies attendees, and adjusts your calendar. No need to dig through calendar apps or send apology emails yourself.

## Group Scheduling

Coordinating across multiple people? Tell Lindy who needs to be there and it finds a time that works for everyone. Lindy checks calendars, proposes options, and locks it in once confirmed.

<Tip>
Lindy automatically accounts for timezone differences when scheduling across locations. You don't need to do the math.
</Tip>

## How to Delegate

- **iMessage / SMS**: "Book a call with Sarah next Tuesday afternoon"
- **Web app**: chat at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai)

<Tip>
Scheduling over text is the fastest option. Lindy already knows your calendar and preferences, so a simple text is all it takes.
</Tip>
