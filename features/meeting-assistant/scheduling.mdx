## CC-Based Scheduling

CC your Lindy email address on any thread where you're trying to find time, and Lindy takes over as your scheduling assistant. It reads the thread, checks your calendar, and handles the rest: no questions, no back-and-forth.

<Steps>
  <Step title="CC Lindy on a scheduling thread">
    Add your Lindy email address to the **CC line** of an email where you and the other person are trying to find a time. The CC is the signal for Lindy to step in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lindy reads the thread and takes over">
    Lindy pulls the details from the thread (duration, date range, time of day, attendees, timezone) and fills any gaps with sensible defaults. If you've already agreed on a time, it books it. If not, it replies once with 3 open slots as clickable links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The meeting gets booked">
    The meeting books automatically on your calendar with invites sent, exactly like Smart Scheduling above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Lindy only acts when **both** are true: your Lindy email is on the CC line, **and** the thread shows scheduling intent ("let's find time", "can we grab 30 min?"). CC without a scheduling ask, or a scheduling ask without the CC, won't trigger it.
</Note>

<Tip>
Want to constrain the options? Just say so in the thread ("mornings only", "next week", "30 min"). Otherwise Lindy uses your standard meeting length, working hours, and buffers.
</Tip>

## Auto Drafting

When someone emails you about scheduling a meeting, Lindy detects the request and offers times in a draft reply on your behalf. Your recipient picks one in a single click and the meeting drops onto both calendars.

- When someone emails to schedule, Lindy offers times in a draft reply
- Recipients book a slot in one click — no back-and-forth
- Save ~3 minutes per invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Someone emails you asking for a time to meet">
    Lindy detects the scheduling request and takes over — no action needed from you.

### Custom Scheduling Rules

Set your scheduling rules in **Settings → Scheduling** in natural language — preferred hours, buffers, blocked times, meeting types, anything that should apply to every auto-drafted reply.

### Add or Update Events on the Fly

Use Lindy to add events to your calendar or change them with one line of text:

- "Add a meeting location"
- "Pick a restaurant for the client dinner"
- "Swap the venue and notify attendees"
- "Move my 2pm to Thursday"
- "Cancel my call with [name]"

Send Lindy a screenshot of a text conversation and she'll pull the meeting details onto the right day.

### End-to-End Outreach

Or send Lindy on a mission and let her run the whole loop:

> "Find time with Bob on Saturday"

She'll reach out, propose times, lock the meeting in, and add it to your calendar.

### Recurring Routines

Set up recurring workflows with one text:

> "Every week, audit my calendar and make sure I have a 45-min walk break at least once a day"

### Internal Calendars

Lindy can check any teammate's calendar that's shared with your workspace, so you can ask her to find time between people on your team without any back-and-forth.
