## How It Works

Just tell Lindy who you want to meet with and when:

- **"Schedule a meeting with [name] next week"**: Lindy proposes times from your calendar and sends a scheduling link
- **"Find 30 minutes for a call with [name] this Thursday"**: Lindy finds your open slots and sends the invite
- **"Schedule a meeting with a screenshot"**: send a screenshot of someone's availability and Lindy will match it against your calendar

## Where to Ask

You can ask Lindy to schedule meetings from anywhere:

- **iMessage / SMS**: "Book a call with Sarah next Tuesday afternoon"
- **Slack**: message Lindy in your workspace
- **Web app**: chat at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai)

<Tip>
Scheduling over text is the fastest option. Lindy already knows your calendar and preferences, so a simple text is all it takes.
</Tip>
