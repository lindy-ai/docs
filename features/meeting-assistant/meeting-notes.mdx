## How to Set It Up

Meeting note-taking is a built-in **routine**. On the **Routines** page, open the **Personal** tab, find the **Meeting note taking** card ("Joins your meetings and takes notes"), and toggle it on. Click the card to set which meetings Lindy joins, the notetaker name, and your summary and recap settings.

## Supported Platforms

Lindy records meetings on **Google Meet**, **Zoom**, and **Microsoft Teams**.

## How It Works

1. **Lindy joins automatically**: no need to invite a bot or share a link. Lindy shows up to your meetings based on your calendar.
2. **Records and transcribes**: Lindy captures the full conversation.
3. **Summarizes and delivers**: after the meeting, Lindy generates a structured summary and sends it to your inbox with a link to the full recording and transcript.

## Which Meetings Lindy Joins

You control which meetings Lindy records:

- **All meetings**: Lindy joins everything on your calendar
- **External only**: record client and prospect calls, skip internal syncs
- **Specific meetings**: choose exactly which meetings to record

## Your Meeting Library

Find all your past recordings under **Home → Meetings** in the left nav. Click any meeting to open the full recording, summary, and transcript.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/meeting recording video.png" alt="Meetings tab in Home with a meeting open showing the recording player and Summary tab" />
</Frame>

Inside a meeting you can:

- **Watch the recording** — adjust playback speed (up to 2x), enter picture-in-picture, or **download** the file
- **Read the summary** — Key Quotes, Action Items, and a high-level summary
- **Browse the transcript** — searchable, timestamped, and clickable to jump to that point in the recording

## Updating Your Meeting Settings

To change any recording settings, open the **Meeting note taking** routine on the **Routines** page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/meeting-note-taking-routine.png" alt="Routines page with an arrow pointing to the Meeting note taking card" />
</Frame>

From here you can:

- Toggle **Meeting recording** on or off
- Set which meetings Lindy joins (**All meetings**, external only, or specific ones)
- Set your **Notetaker display name** — what Lindy is called when it joins
- Choose who receives the **post-meeting follow-up email**
- Define your **Summarization instructions** — structure, tone, and level of detail
- Choose where you're notified (email, Slack, etc.)

## Chat With Your Meetings

Your meeting library becomes a knowledge base you can query in plain language — via iMessage, SMS, or directly in the dashboard.

- "What did we decide in last week's meeting with Sarah?"
- "What were the action items from Tuesday's standup?"
- "What did [name] say about pricing in our last call?"
- "What did I commit to sending [name]?"

## Post-Meeting Follow-ups

After every meeting, Lindy extracts the action items and sends a follow-up email to attendees. Decisions get documented, next steps are clear, and everyone knows what they own.

1. **Meeting ends**: Lindy processes the recording and transcript
2. **Action items extracted**: Lindy identifies decisions, next steps, and owners
3. **Follow-up sent**: an email goes out to attendees with the summary and action items

Set who receives it in the **Meeting note taking** routine:

- **All participants**: everyone on the invite
- **Internal only**: just your team
- **Specific people**: customize per your preferences

### Custom Follow-up Actions

Beyond email, tell Lindy to take extra actions after meetings. In the **Meeting note taking** routine, use the **Follow-up Actions** field to describe what Lindy should do.

<Note>
  **Lindy works with your existing tools** — Notion, Slack, Todoist, and hundreds more. Just tell it what to do (*"Add action items to Todoist"*) and it handles the rest. The first time it uses an app, Lindy asks you to connect it.
</Note>

## Quick Setup

1. On the **Routines** page, open the **Personal** tab and click the **Meeting note taking** card
2. Toggle the routine on
3. Connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook calendar
4. Set your notetaker display name and summarization instructions
5. Lindy joins and records from your next scheduled meeting on Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams
