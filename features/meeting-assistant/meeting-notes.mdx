Lindy automatically joins your meetings, records them, and delivers an interactive, searchable summary afterwards. You get the key decisions, action items, and a full transcript without typing a single note.

## How It Works

Enable meeting recording in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Meeting recording** in the settings panel. Once on:

1. **Lindy joins automatically**: no need to invite a bot or share a link. Lindy shows up to your meetings based on your calendar.
2. **Records and transcribes**: Lindy captures the full conversation.
3. **Summarizes**: after the meeting, Lindy generates a structured summary and sends it to you.

## Which Meetings Lindy Joins

You can configure which meetings Lindy records:

- **All meetings**: Lindy joins everything on your calendar
- **External only**: skip internal syncs
- **Specific meetings**: customize based on your preferences

## Summary Structure

Lindy's meeting summaries follow a clear structure that you can customize:

- **High-level summary**: max 3 bullet points with bolded key words and phrases
- **Detailed summary**: in-depth bullet points covering the full discussion
- **Action items**: who needs to do what, with clear ownership
- **Link to task**: direct link to the full transcript and recording

You can edit the **Summarization Instructions** in your settings to match your preferred format, tone, and level of detail.

## Where Notes Are Delivered

Meeting summaries are sent via **email** after the meeting ends. The email includes the summary plus a link to the full recording and transcript in your Lindy dashboard.
