## Research

Lindy combines your **work context** (emails, meetings, calendar) with **deep web research** to give you thorough, relevant answers.

- "Research [company] before my meeting tomorrow": Lindy pulls company info, recent news, and cross-references with your past interactions
- "What's the latest on [industry topic]?": web research grounded in what's relevant to your work
- "Summarize this article": send any URL and get the key points back

Because Lindy knows who you're meeting with and what you've discussed, research is always personalized to your context.

## Knowledge Bank

Lindy remembers everything from your past emails and meetings. Ask questions anytime:

- "What did [name] say about pricing in our last call?"
- "Find the email where [name] sent the contract"
- "When was my last meeting with [company]?"
- "What were the action items from last week's team sync?"

## How to Ask

Lindy is available wherever you work:

| Channel | Best for |
|---------|----------|
| **iMessage / SMS** | Quick asks on the go. [See full guide](/features/imessage-sms) |
| **Slack** | When you're already in Slack and want a fast answer |
| **Web app** | Longer tasks, reviewing results at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai) |

<Tip>
Texting Lindy is the fastest way to get things done. Lindy already has context on your email, calendar, and past conversations.
</Tip>
