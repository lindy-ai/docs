Beyond running your inbox and meetings, Lindy handles one-off tasks whenever you need something done. Just ask.

## Research

Lindy combines your **work context** (emails, meetings, calendar) with **deep web research** to give you thorough, relevant answers.

- **Prep for a meeting**: "Research [company] before my meeting tomorrow." Lindy pulls company info, recent news, and cross-references with your past interactions
- **Industry research**: "What's the latest on [industry topic]?" Web research grounded in what's relevant to your work
- **Summarize content**: Send any URL or YouTube link and get the key points back
- **Competitive intel**: "What is [company] announcing this quarter?" Lindy finds public info and connects it to your deals and conversations
- **People research**: "What do I know about [name]?" Lindy pulls from your emails, meetings, and the web

## Knowledge Bank

Lindy remembers everything from your past emails and meetings. Ask questions anytime:

- **Recall conversations**: "What did [name] say about pricing in our last call?"
- **Find emails**: "Find the email where [name] sent the contract"
- **Check history**: "When was my last meeting with [company]?"
- **Surface action items**: "What were the action items from last week's team sync?"
- **Track commitments**: "What did I promise [name] I'd send them?"
