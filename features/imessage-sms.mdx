## Text Lindy, Anywhere

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/imessage-hero.jpg" alt="iMessage conversation with Lindy showing delegation over text" />
</Frame>

Lindy works over both **iMessage** and **SMS**, so you can delegate to your AI assistant right from your phone. No app to install. No dashboard to check. Just text it and it's done.

## Getting Set Up

Add your phone number during [signup](/start-here/quickstart) and verify with a confirmation code. That's it. Lindy sends you your first message, already personalized with context from your email and calendar. Just text back.

<Tip>
If you skipped the phone number step during signup, you can add it anytime in your [account settings](https://chat.lindy.ai).
</Tip>

## What You Can Delegate

### Calendar & Meetings

- **Schedule a meeting**: "Set up a 30-min call with Sarah next Tuesday"
- **Schedule with a screenshot**: Send a screenshot of someone's availability and Lindy will find a time that works
- **Ask about past meetings**: "What did we decide in last week's standup?"
- **Prep for your next meeting**: "Brief me on my 2pm meeting"

### Email

- **Let someone know you're running late**: "Tell my next meeting I'll be 5 minutes late"
- **Set up email alerts**: "Text me when I get an email from [name]"
- **Search your inbox**: "Find the last email from [name] about the proposal"

### Quick Tasks

- **Summarize a YouTube video**: Send a link and Lindy gives you the key points
- **Summarize web content**: Send any URL for a quick summary
- **Find contact information**: "What's [name]'s email?"
- **Work with your CRM**: "Add [name] to my CRM" or "Pull up [name]'s deal"
- **Voice notes**: Send a voice note and Lindy transcribes and acts on it. You can also turn voice notes into Notion pages (requires Notion to be connected via text)

### Time & Priorities

- **Audit your time**: "How did I spend my time this week?"
- **Prioritize your day**: "What should I focus on today?"
- **React to changes**: "My 3pm cancelled, what should I do with that time?"

### Recurring Tasks

- **Daily briefings**: "Send me a briefing every morning at 7am" (includes your calendar, important emails, weather, and news)
- **Reminders**: "Remind me to follow up with [name] on Friday"
- **Standing instructions**: "Every time I finish a meeting, send the notes to Slack"
- **Weekly recaps**: "Send me a summary of my week every Friday at 5pm"
- **CRM updates after meetings**: "Update the CRM after every sales call"

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/daily-briefing.jpg" alt="Daily morning briefing from Lindy via iMessage" />
</Frame>

You can customize recurring tasks like briefings in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Daily briefings default to **7:00 AM**.

## Troubleshooting

If you're not receiving text messages, ensure your **Preferred Messaging Service** is aligned with your device settings. To update this, in Lindy's web UI, go to **Settings → General → Preferred Messaging Service**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/preferred-messaging.png" alt="Preferred Messaging Service setting on iPhone" />
</Frame>

The setting defaults to **auto**, but you can override it to match your mobile device. If you're on Android and want to use RCS, open the **Google Messages** app, tap your profile picture, go to **Settings → RCS chats**, and turn on **"Turn on RCS chats"**.
