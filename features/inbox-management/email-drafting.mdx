## Replies That Sound Like You

</Frame>

Lindy drafts email responses using your tone, style, and preferences. Every draft lands in your drafts folder for you to review, edit, and send. Nothing goes out without your approval.

## How It Works

Enable email drafting in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Email drafting** in the settings panel. Once on, Lindy will:

1. Read incoming emails that need a response
2. Draft a reply based on your drafting instructions
3. Save it as a draft in your email client
4. You review, tweak if needed, and hit send

## Drafting Instructions

Tell Lindy how you write. In your settings, you'll see a text field for **Drafting Instructions** where you describe your style.

Example:
> Match the sender's tone and formality level. Keep responses concise (under 3 sentences if possible). Sign off with 'Best' for professional contacts and 'Thanks' for casual ones. Never draft responses to no-reply emails.

The more specific you are, the better Lindy's drafts will sound. You can mention:
- **Tone**: formal, casual, friendly, direct
- **Length**: short and punchy, or thorough and detailed
- **Sign-offs**: "Best," "Thanks," "Cheers," your custom signature
- **Rules**: who to never reply to, what topics to escalate

<Tip>
Lindy learns from your feedback. If a draft isn't quite right, edit it before sending and Lindy will adapt over time.
</Tip>

## "Sent from Lindy" Signature

You can optionally add a **"Sent from Lindy"** signature to drafts. This is off by default. Toggle it in your settings if you want recipients to know your AI assistant helped with the reply.

## Automatic Follow-ups

Lindy can also draft follow-up emails when someone hasn't replied to you. Set the timing in your settings. The default is **2 days**.

<Frame caption="TODO: Screenshot of follow-up timing setting">
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/what-is-lindy-banner.jpg" alt="Placeholder" />
</Frame>

When enabled, Lindy will:
- Track emails you've sent that haven't received a reply
- After the set number of days, draft a follow-up
- Save it in your drafts for your review

This makes sure nothing falls through the cracks. You always have the final say before anything sends.
