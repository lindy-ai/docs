## Replies That Sound Like You

After [Email Triage](/features/inbox-management/email-triage) sorts your inbox, Lindy drafts replies for emails that need a response. Every draft lands in your drafts folder for you to review, edit, and send. Nothing goes out without your approval.

## How to Set It Up

<Note>
  Open Lindy, click your **workspace name** in the top-left, and choose **Settings**. In the left sidebar, click **Emails**, then scroll to **Email drafting**.
</Note>

## How It Works

Enable email drafting in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Email drafting** in the settings panel. Once on, Lindy will:

1. Read incoming emails that need a response
2. Draft a reply based on your drafting instructions
3. Save it as a draft in your email client

From there, you review, tweak if needed, and hit send.

## Drafting Instructions

Lindy drafts from two sets of instructions, both in **Settings → Emails → Email drafting**.

### Learned instructions

Lindy picks these up from how you write and handle emails, and they get better and better the more you use Lindy. Open them any time to correct or add to what Lindy has learned, then click **Save**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/learned-instructions.png" alt="Learned instructions panel showing common phrases Lindy picked up from the user, with a Save button" />
</Frame>

### Drafting instructions

This is your own field. Paste or write your style rules and Lindy follows them. For example:
> Match the sender's tone and formality level. Keep responses concise (under 3 sentences if possible). Sign off with 'Best' for professional contacts and 'Thanks' for casual ones. Never draft responses to no-reply emails.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/drafting-instructions.png" alt="Drafting instructions text field with example tone and style rules" />
</Frame>

The more specific you are, the better Lindy's drafts will sound. You can mention:
- **Tone**: formal, casual, friendly, direct
- **Length**: short and punchy, or thorough and detailed
- **Sign-offs**: "Best," "Thanks," "Cheers," your custom signature
- **Rules**: who to never reply to, what topics to escalate

Your learned instructions keep improving from how you write, while your drafting instructions stay exactly as you set them.

## Signature

Customize what shows up at the bottom of every Lindy draft.

**Your signature** is your standard sign-off, rendered above the Lindy line. Add plain text or markdown — paste in your Gmail signature, or write something new.

**Include Sent with Lindy** adds a small "Sent with Lindy" tagline below your signature. It's on by default — toggle it off in settings if you'd rather not include it.

## Automatic Follow-ups

Lindy tracks emails you've sent that haven't gotten a reply. After a set number of days (default: **2 days**), Lindy drafts a follow-up and saves it in your drafts for review. Adjust the timing in your settings. You always have the final say before anything sends.
