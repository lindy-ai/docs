Lindy automatically organizes your inbox by labeling and sorting emails into categories. Important messages stay front and center. Everything else gets filed away. No rules to write, no filters to manage.

## Email Labeling

Enable email labeling in your settings at [chat.lindy.ai](https://chat.lindy.ai). Look for **Email labeling** in the settings panel. Once enabled, Lindy reads every incoming email and assigns it to the right category.

### Categories

Lindy splits your email into two groups:

**Move out of your inbox**: these get archived and labeled so they're out of sight:
- Calendar invites and updates
- Promotions and marketing emails

**Keep in your inbox**: these stay visible with a label:

| Label | What it catches |
|-------|----------------|
| **To Respond** | Emails that need a reply from you |
| **FYI** | Informational, no action needed |
| **Newsletters** | Subscriptions and recurring content |
| **Comments** | Mentions, comments, and notifications from tools |
| **Notifications** | System alerts and automated messages |
| **Invoices** | Bills, receipts, and payment-related emails |
| **Lindy** | Messages from Lindy (meeting notes, briefings, etc.) |

## Urgent Email Alerts

Lindy can **text you** when something time-sensitive hits your inbox. Enable "Alert me by text for time-sensitive emails" in your settings and customize what counts as urgent.

<Frame caption="TODO: Screenshot of urgent email alert settings">
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/what-is-lindy-banner.jpg" alt="Placeholder" />
</Frame>

For example:
- "Only text me about meetings that are within 30 minutes, or emails that seem extremely urgent"
- "Alert me when I get an email from [VIP client name]"
- "Text me if someone replies to the proposal thread"

<Tip>
This pairs well with [iMessage & SMS](/features/imessage-sms). You get notified on your phone and can respond to Lindy right there.
</Tip>

## How It Works

Lindy doesn't rely on static rules or keyword matching. It autonomously reads each email and understands the context: who sent it, what it's about, and whether it needs your attention. The more you use Lindy, the better it gets at understanding what matters to you.
