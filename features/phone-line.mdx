Lindy can have its own phone number. Call it to talk through your day or hand off a task, ask it to call you, or let it handle a call on your behalf. You decide how it answers other people and what information it can share.

## Set up your phone line

1. Verify your personal phone number in **Settings → General**. This is how Lindy recognizes you and knows where to call you.
2. Open **Settings → Phone numbers** and select **Get a number**. Choose a US or Canadian area code and confirm the details shown. If you have an eligible unused Lindy number, you can reuse it.
3. Call the **Lindy number shown at the top of Phone numbers** from your verified personal phone. Try: “Walk me through my day.”

Your personal number and Lindy’s number have different jobs: **you call Lindy’s number; Lindy calls your verified personal number**.

A paid plan is required, and availability may vary by account. A newly purchased number has a monthly fee, and calls use credits. Check the current details in the app before confirming. See [Usage & Limits](/account-billing/usage).

## Choose a voice

In **Settings → Phone numbers**, open the **Voice** dropdown. Click the play button beside a voice to hear a sample, then select the voice you want Lindy to use for incoming and outgoing calls.

## Talk to your assistant

When you call from your verified number, Lindy can help with your connected apps, calendar, files, and saved context. For example:

- “What do I need to prepare for my next meeting?”
- “Find the document we discussed yesterday.”
- “Help me work through this decision.”

What Lindy can do depends on your connected tools and permissions. If a task needs an approval or another account connection, complete that step in chat. Longer work can continue there after the call.

To have Lindy call you, select **Call me** in the chat composer or ask in chat: “Call me and interview me about this project.” Your verified personal phone will ring.

## Have Lindy make a call for you

Tell Lindy who to call, their number, what you want done, and what information it can share. Include acceptable alternatives and what to do if nobody answers.

> Call the dentist to move my appointment to Thursday afternoon. Friday morning also works. Share my name and current appointment time. If neither works, ask for the next available afternoon. If it goes to voicemail, leave my callback number.

Review the destination and purpose when Lindy asks for approval. It calls from its own number and uses the instructions you provided, without giving the other person access to your private assistant context.

Lindy can navigate phone menus, wait on hold, and leave a voicemail. After the call, it reports what happened in chat. For recurring calls, include the schedule and call instructions in a [routine](/teammate/routines).

## Let Lindy answer for you

Open **When others call** in **Settings → Phone numbers** to set up how Lindy handles incoming calls.

- **Instructions:** Explain how Lindy should introduce itself, what to ask, and what it may share.
- **Messages:** Lindy can take a caller’s name, reason for calling, and contact details, then pass the message to you.
- **Book meetings:** Enable booking and select an active booking link to let callers choose from its available slots.

Outside callers cannot access your inbox, files, memory, or private conversations. Booking gives them access to available slots on the selected link, without exposing the rest of your calendar.

## Manage your line

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Require a PIN** | Under **When you call**, add a four-digit PIN for access to your personal assistant. |
| **Only I can call** | Under **When others call**, allow calls only from your verified phone. |
| **Blocked numbers** | Prevent selected numbers from reaching your line. |
| **Active** | Pause incoming and outgoing calls. Pausing does not cancel the number’s monthly fee. |

The overview under your number shows this month’s call usage. Calls are recorded, and you can review the associated chat or task afterward. Regional, usage, and calling-hours limits apply; Lindy will let you know if a requested call cannot proceed.

## Disconnect or release a number

In **Settings → Phone numbers**, open the three-dot menu beside your number.

- **Disconnect** unlinks the number from your assistant while keeping the number in your account.
- **Release** gives up the number permanently. Choose this when you no longer want to keep it.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why didn’t Lindy recognize me?">
    Call from the personal number verified in **Settings → General**. Lindy recognizes your calling number, not your voice. A call from a different number gets the restricted experience for other callers. If you enabled a PIN, enter it when prompted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change caller instructions in chat?">
    Yes. Ask Lindy to update your caller instructions, voice, or blocked numbers, then review the proposed change before approving it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does pausing my line stop the monthly charge?">
    No. The **Active** switch pauses calls. To give up the number, use **Release** from its three-dot menu in **Settings → Phone numbers**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
