Booking links let people choose an available time on your calendar. Create different links for different meetings, such as a quick intro or a longer client call, and share the one that fits.

## Before you start

Connect your **Google Calendar** in [Integrations](/integrations/overview), with permission for Lindy to create events. Booking links use your own connected calendar. If you have several Google accounts, you can choose which one a link books on.

## Create and share a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booking links">
    Open **Meetings**, find **Booking links**, and click **Create custom**. You can also start with one of the suggested meeting types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the meeting">
    Enter a title and description, then choose the meeting length and video app. Use your usual video app, Google Meet, connected Zoom, or no video link.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Meeting length">
        <div style={{ maxWidth: '340px', margin: '0 auto' }}>
          <Frame>
            <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/booking-links/meeting-length.png" alt="Meeting length options: 15, 30, or 45 minutes, 1 hour, or 1.5 hours" />
          </Frame>

      <Tab title="Video app">

          <Frame>
            <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/booking-links/video-app.png" alt="Video app options: your usual video app, Google Meet, Zoom, or no video link" />

    </Tabs>

    Review **Availability** and **Settings**, then click **Create link**. Each link can have its own hours and meeting length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and share">
    Click **Copy link** on the meeting card. Paste it into a message, your email signature, or your website. Open the link yourself to check what guests will see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adjust availability and settings

Click a meeting card to edit it. Make your changes, then click **Save**.

## What guests see

Guests choose a date and time, then enter their name and email, with an optional note. They don't need a Lindy account. Times display in their selected time zone, and the booking creates a calendar event.

Their confirmation includes a link to reschedule or cancel. Lindy checks availability again before confirming a new time.

## Pause or limit a link

Turn off the switch on a meeting card to stop new bookings. Existing meetings stay booked. Turn it back on when you're ready.

For a link that should accept one booking, enable **Single-use** in **Settings**. It turns off after the first booking.
