### Installation Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Lindy Account">
    Visit [chat.lindy.ai/signup](https://chat.lindy.ai/signup) to create your free account.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    In the Lindy dashboard, navigate to Integrations and click "Connect Slack". Authorize Lindy to access your workspace.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Configure Your Bot">
    Choose which channels to monitor, set up filters, and connect your knowledge base.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Test by mentioning Lindy or using your configured trigger phrases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Can Lindy Do in Slack?

- **Answer team questions instantly** using your internal documents and knowledge base
- **Automate customer support** by routing and responding to inquiries
- **Send smart notifications** from your CRM, calendar, or other tools
- **Summarize conversations** and extract action items
- **Route messages** to the right team members based on content

## Common Use Cases

### Internal Knowledge Bot
Answer employee questions about policies, procedures, and company information using your HR documents, handbooks, and wikis.

### Customer Support Assistant
Respond to customer inquiries in shared Slack channels, pulling information from your help docs and FAQ.

### Sales Alert System
Get instant notifications when new leads arrive, deals close, or important CRM events occur.

### Meeting Notes Distribution
Automatically share meeting summaries and action items to relevant channels after calls end.
