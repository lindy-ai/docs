## AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

<Warning>
**Important Notice About AI Responses**

Lindy's bot for Slack uses artificial intelligence to generate responses based on your configured knowledge base and instructions. While we strive for accuracy, AI-generated content may occasionally:

- Contain inaccuracies or outdated information
- Misinterpret the context or intent of messages
- Provide incomplete or partially correct answers
- Generate responses that require human verification

**We recommend:**
- Reviewing AI responses for critical or sensitive topics
- Maintaining an up-to-date knowledge base for best accuracy
- Setting up escalation paths to human team members when needed
- Using the "Human in the Loop" feature for high-stakes decisions

Lindy does not use your Slack data to train AI models. Your data remains private and secure.
</Warning>
