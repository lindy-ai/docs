## What is a Workflow?

Lindy's assistant handles your inbox, meetings, and calendar out of the box. Workflows go further: you build your own automations for anything else, like lead enrichment, support bots, document processing, and more.

## How to Create a Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Workflow menu">
    In the **top left** of your Lindy dashboard, click the chevron (▾) next to **+ New chat** to open the dropdown. Select **New workflow**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/new-workflow-dropdown.jpg" alt="Click the chevron next to New chat and select New workflow" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a template or start from scratch">
    You'll see a modal with popular templates like Meeting Notetaker, Lead Generator, and Email Responder. Pick one to get started quickly, or click **Start from scratch** to build your own from a blank canvas.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/new-workflow-modal.jpg" alt="Choose from templates or start from scratch" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
    Click **Explore templates** to browse the full template gallery.
    </Tip>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/new-workflow-templates.jpg" alt="Template gallery with popular agents filtered by role and use case" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a trigger">
    You're now in the **flow editor**. Start by adding a [trigger](/fundamentals/lindy-101/triggers): the event that wakes up your agent and kicks off the workflow. Click the plus sign or right-click on the canvas and select "Add Trigger."

    Common triggers: new email received, new calendar event, scheduled time, chat message, webhook, and more.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add actions and configure your workflow">
    After your trigger, add [actions](/fundamentals/lindy-101/actions): the steps your agent takes in response. Connect integrations, add [conditions](/fundamentals/lindy-101/conditions) for branching logic, or use [agent steps](/fundamentals/lindy-101/ai-agents) for tasks that need AI reasoning.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate your workflow">
    Once your workflow is ready, toggle it on. It will start running automatically whenever the trigger fires. You can monitor runs in the [Tasks view](/testing/monitoring-your-agents).
  </Step>
</Steps>
