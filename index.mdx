Lindy is an AI employee that takes on work across the tools you already use. Ask it to draft a client update from a spreadsheet, pull next steps from a meeting, or get your morning brief ready. When a job repeats, set up a routine so Lindy can run it on a schedule.

## Give Lindy your first job

Tell Lindy what you want back and which files or tools to use. Review what it brings back and ask for any changes in the same conversation. Try one of the prompts in [Use cases](/use-cases) or follow the [Quickstart](/start-here/quickstart) to get set up.

## Reuse context and instructions

Lindy keeps useful context from your work so you don't have to explain everything again. Reuse skills and routines, and share meeting folders and files so your team can build on the same knowledge.

You choose what stays personal and what your team can access.

## Where you can use Lindy

**For your own work**, talk to Lindy privately in the web app, iMessage, email, or a Slack DM.

**For shared work**, @mention Lindy in a Slack channel. It can use shared meetings, files, and connected tools, and anyone in the thread can follow along.
