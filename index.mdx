## The AI teammate that knows your whole company

Lindy Teammate lives in Slack and knows everything your company knows. Everyone gets a private Lindy in their DMs. The whole team shares one in channels.

## It does real work, right in Slack

## Use cases

Not just quick answers. Lindy helps your team tackle more.

### Find answers

Ask across Slack, meetings, and your tools. Lindy digs up the answer and the file or decision behind it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/find-answers.png" alt="Lindy in #product: finds the Figma link for the new homepage, and confirms the pricing decision from last week's review with the meeting and Notion log" />
</Frame>

### Build decks

Hand Lindy the deck. It pulls from your meetings and tools and comes back with slides, charts, and commentary, ready to present.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/build-decks.png" alt="Lindy in #leadership: asked to bring together the all-hands deck, it uses this month's shared meetings and Slack, and returns a 12-slide Google Slides deck ready to present" />
</Frame>

### Investigate

Ask why a number moved. Lindy traces it across analytics, code, and tickets to the root cause, with the fix ready.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/investigate.png" alt="Lindy in #growth: asked why signups are down, it finds mobile signups dropped 61% after Tuesday's deploy, and the one-line CSS fix is already in" />
</Frame>

### Pull reports

The daily or weekly numbers, pulled from your ad, CRM, and finance tools into a live report, with anything off-target flagged.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/pull-reports.png" alt="Lindy posts a daily ad snapshot: $8.4k across Meta and Google, ROAS 3.1 over target, with retargeting flagged at 2.1x target CPA" />
</Frame>

### Handle busywork

The CRM updates, the handoff doc, the follow-ups. Lindy runs the multi-step work nobody wants to.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/handle-busywork.png" alt="Lindy runs a deal-won handoff: updates HubSpot, creates the Notion doc, posts to customer-success, schedules the kickoff, and sends the welcome email" />
</Frame>

### Catch up

Been out, or dropped into a busy channel? Lindy gives you the TL;DR and exactly what needs you.

<Frame>
  <img src="/lindy-brand-assets/teammate/catch-up.png" alt="Lindy summarizes the #launch channel: launch moved to Thursday, design and legal signed off, and flags that you need to approve the email by tomorrow" />
