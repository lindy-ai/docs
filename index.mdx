Lindy is an AI employee that completes work across your connected tools. It acts as a personal executive assistant for you and a shared teammate for your team.

**For you**, Lindy helps manage your inbox and calendar, prepares you for meetings, records calls, and handles follow-ups and other tasks. You can work with it privately through iMessage, email, or Slack DMs.

**For your team**, @mention Lindy in a shared Slack channel to research a question, create a report, or update your CRM. Lindy uses shared meetings, files, and connected tools, and teammates in the conversation can follow along.

Lindy can also work proactively through routines, such as sending your morning brief, flagging important emails, or running recurring tasks.

## Give Lindy a task

Describe what you need in a conversation, such as a client report, a dashboard, or a summary of a customer meeting.

It brings together context from your connected tools, meetings, and files to complete the task. Review the result, ask for changes, and continue the work in the same conversation.

## Reuse context and instructions

Lindy keeps useful context from your work so you don't have to explain everything again. Reuse skills and routines, and share meeting folders and files so your team can build on the same knowledge.

You choose what stays personal and what your team can access.
