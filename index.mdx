## The AI teammate that knows your whole company

Lindy Teammate lives in Slack and knows everything your company knows. Everyone gets a private assistant in their DMs. The whole team shares one in channels.

<Icon icon="magnifying-glass" iconType="solid" /> **Ask.** Answers from your tools and every meeting.
