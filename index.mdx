## The AI teammate that makes the whole team better

Lindy Teammate lives in Slack and knows everything your company knows. Everyone gets a private Lindy in their DMs. The whole team shares one in channels.

Most teams already have AI, and almost none of it adds up: private chat histories, one person's clever setup living in one person's tab, everyone starting from zero every time.

Lindy is the one they share. Skills, routines, and recorded meetings belong to the workspace, not to whoever set them up, so one person teaching it something leaves the whole team better at their job.

## Finished work, not just an answer

Ask from Slack and Lindy doesn't hand you something to go do. It updates the forecast tab, writes the recap doc, enriches the contact record, files the notes where the team already looks. Write actions always wait for your approval, so nothing ships without you.

## Skills it has, and the ones you teach it

A skill is a named playbook: *how I write my weekly report*, *how we qualify a lead*. Describe a process once and Lindy reaches for it on its own, without you invoking anything.
