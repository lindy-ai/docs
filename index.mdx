Lindy is an AI teammate that gets work done for you and your team.

For you, it handles email, meeting prep, scheduling, and follow-ups.

For your team, it creates reports, builds dashboards, updates your CRM, and answers questions using context from shared meetings, files, and connected tools.

Work with Lindy privately, or @mention it in Slack so your team can follow along. Start by connecting the tools you need and handing off one task.

## Finished work, not just an answer

Ask Lindy for a finished result: a client report, a dashboard, or a customer handoff.

It brings together context from your connected tools, meetings, and files to complete the task. Review the result, ask for changes, and continue the work in the same conversation.

## Context that compounds

Lindy keeps useful context from your work so you don't have to explain everything again. Reuse skills and routines, and share meeting folders and files so your team can build on the same knowledge.

You choose what stays personal and what your team can access.
