## Finished work, not just an answer

Ask from Slack and Lindy doesn't hand you something to go do. One request pulls the week's numbers from Snowflake, traces the change that caused them in GitHub, enriches the contact record in Salesforce, drafts the summary in Gmail, files the notes in Notion where the team already looks, and posts the answer back in #growth.

Write actions always wait for your approval, so nothing ships without you.
